\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The tenth review broke round 15 at its designated keystone W1a: the
steering condition (Q4) was \emph{pairwise} --- each unrepresented pair
must admit a safe horizontal value in its fold --- while closure is a
\emph{joint} constraint, and a minimal machine-checked witness (safe
sets $\{\PO\}$ and $\{\DR\}$ over a realized $\PP$ edge, with
$\Comp(\PP,\DR)=\{\DR\}$) passes the pairwise condition although no
joint completion exists.  This delta adopts the review's own repair
direction and makes the joint selection part of the certificate: every
gluing edge carries a \emph{steering function} from interface
\emph{states} (type, row over the separator, safety signature) to fresh
rows, subject to four finite conditions (through-separator, safety,
fresh-edge, and a pair condition over the reachable pair states with
their realized mutual values).  The canonical completion of the
soundness proof becomes \emph{deterministic}: every cross value in the
unfolding is generated by the certified functions, so no pointwise
choice remains anywhere in the proof, and each of the four triangle
classes is discharged by a certified condition.  The tenth review's
witness is rejected (no steering function exists), and rejection is
semantically correct: the witness configuration is unrealizable, so the
old (Q4) accepted garbage and the repair provably cannot lose
completeness on that family.  The honest cost is stated and measured:
extraction must now produce \emph{uniform} steering data from a model,
and on random glue steps a state-uniform function exists in $98.6\%$
of jointly-realizable instances (WP28) --- the residual
\emph{uniformization} obligation is this round's designated keystone.
Status: \emph{strongly supported, not certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the tenth review established, and what stands}\label{sec:pos}

Withdrawn from round 15: condition (Q4) and Lemma 4.3's pointwise
canonical completion.  The review's witness is minimal: old pattern
$y\,\PP\,z\,\PP\,s$ (closed), fresh pattern $s\,\PPI\,b$; both folds are
$\Rel$; $\Safe(Y,B)=\{\PO\}$ and $\Safe(Z,B)=\{\DR\}$ realized by
explicit universals.  (Q4)(b) passes pairwise --- for $(y,b)$ the
selector $\DR$ is unsafe but the other horizontal $\PO$ is safe --- yet
any assignment must satisfy
$\rho(y,b)\in\Comp(\rho(y,z),\rho(z,b))=\Comp(\PP,\DR)=\{\DR\}$,
unreachable from $\{\PO\}$.  The round-15 proof conflated
pre-assignment feasibility (membership in the fold) with the
post-assignment constraint (the composition cell fixed by the
neighbour's chosen value).  This was the project's recurring
pointwise-versus-joint failure genus, fourth incarnation.

Two observations sharpen the repair.  First, the witness configuration
is \emph{semantically unrealizable}: $y\subset z$ and $z\cap b=\varnothing$
force $y\,\DR\,b$, which $\Safe(Y,B)$ forbids; so a correct certificate
condition must \emph{reject} it --- (Q4) was a soundness hole, and a
strictly stronger condition cannot lose completeness on such
configurations, because extraction from a real model always has the
joint solution in hand.  Second, the review left the surrounding
architecture unattacked: the fold lattice, one-step demands (Q2), the
core (Q5), and the width measure were not reached (not exonerated ---
see Section~\ref{sec:invalidate}).  This delta therefore changes exactly
one layer: the steering condition and the completion proof that consumes
it.  We also repair the (Q2) wording instability the review noted in
passing (Section~\ref{sec:q2}).

\section{Interface states and certified steering}\label{sec:steer}

Fix a cluster quasimodel candidate $Q$ as in round-15
Definition 3.3, with (Q4) deleted.  For a gluing edge
$g=(k'\!\to\!k,\sigma)$ of the catalogue (a fresh copy of pattern $k$
glued on separator $\sigma$), write $B_g$ for the fresh members of $k$
and $N_k$ for its network.

\begin{definition}[Interface state]\label{def:state}
The \emph{state} of an ambient occurrence $y$ at a gluing $g$ is
\[
\St_g(y)\;=\;\bigl(\tp(y),\ \rho\restriction(\{y\}\times\sigma),\
(\Safe(\tp y,\tau_k b))_{b\in B_g}\bigr):
\]
its type, its row of realized values over the separator, and its safety
signature toward the fresh members.  States range over the finite set
$\Typ(C_0)\times\Rel^{|\sigma|}\times(2^{\Rel})^{|B_g|}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Steering function; condition (Q4$'$)]\label{def:q4p}
The certificate carries, for every gluing edge $g$, a partial function
$f_g$ from states to fresh rows, $f_g(\varsigma)\in\Rel^{B_g}$, whose
domain contains all $g$-reachable states (Definition~\ref{def:reach}),
subject to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(S\arabic*),leftmargin=2.6em]
\item \textbf{Through-separator:} $f_g(\varsigma)(b)\in
\Comp\bigl(r(s),N_k(s,b)\bigr)$ for every $s\in\sigma$, where $r$ is the
row of $\varsigma$.
\item \textbf{Safety:} $f_g(\varsigma)(b)\in\Safe(t,\tau_k b)$, where
$t$ is the type of $\varsigma$; never $\EQ$.
\item \textbf{Fresh edges:} $f_g(\varsigma)(b')\in
\Comp\bigl(f_g(\varsigma)(b),N_k(b,b')\bigr)$ for all $b,b'\in B_g$.
\item \textbf{Pair condition:} for every $g$-reachable pair
$(\varsigma,\varsigma',W)$ --- two states with a realized mutual value
$W$ (Definition~\ref{def:reach}) --- and every $b\in B_g$:
$f_g(\varsigma)(b)\in\Comp\bigl(W,f_g(\varsigma')(b)\bigr)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reachability; the certification fixed point]
\label{def:reach}
Because every cross value in the unfolding will be generated by the
$f_g$'s (Theorem~\ref{thm:canon}), the set of states and pairs that can
actually occur is \emph{computable from the certificate itself}: a
least fixed point over the finite data
\[
\bigl(\text{pattern member types and networks}\bigr)\times
\bigl(\text{rows evolving by } r\mapsto
r\!\restriction\!\text{continued}\ \cup\ f_g\text{-values}\bigr)\times
\bigl(\text{mutual values } W\text{ generated in-pattern or by }f\bigr).
\]
Concretely: singleton states are seeded by pattern members with their
in-pattern rows; crossing a gluing updates a row by restriction to
continued separator members and extension by the $f$-values; pair
states are seeded by co-patterned pairs with their network values and
extended by $f$-generated mutual values (an old/fresh pair's $W$ is the
$f$-value assigned when the fresh member was created).  All components
range over finite sets, so the fixed point is finite and effective.
(Q4$'$) is checked exactly on its reachable part.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
This is literally the tenth review's closing recommendation --- ``the
certificate must store a joint row/profile selection, not merely
certify that each individual pair has some safe horizontal value'' ---
implemented with the selection made \emph{functional on states} so that
the reachability analysis closes finitely.  The fold lattice of round
15 is no longer the load-bearing device (rows and $f$-values supersede
per-pair folds); it survives as the a-priori bound showing row
components and $W$'s live in a small algebra, and Facts 2.1--2.2 of
round 15 remain the toolkit for the width and extraction sections.
\end{remark}

\section{The canonical completion, re-proved}\label{sec:canon}

\begin{theorem}[Deterministic canonical completion]\label{thm:canon}
Let $Q$ satisfy (Q1)--(Q3), (Q5), (Q6) of round 15 and (Q4$'$).  Then
the unfolding $U(Q)$ carries a total complete atomic composition-closed
relation $\rho$ with $\EQ$ only on the diagonal, extending every
pattern value, in which every non-co-patterned pair's value is
$f$-generated; and every such value lies in
$\Safe$ of its endpoint types.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Process the unfolding in tree order, one pattern copy per step, the
core inside the base as in round 15.  Invariant: the realized structure
is closed, atomic, extends all pattern values so far, every
non-co-patterned value is $f$-generated and safe, and every ambient
occurrence's state at the next gluing --- and every ambient pair's
$(\varsigma,\varsigma',W)$ --- lies in the certified reachable sets.

At a step gluing a copy of $k$ over $\sigma$: assign, for every ambient
$y$ and fresh $b$,
\[
\rho(y,b):=f_g\bigl(\St_g(y)\bigr)(b).
\]
There are no choices.  Closure of the extended structure is a check of
four triangle classes, each discharged by a certified condition:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item $(y,s,b)$, $s\in\sigma$: by (S1), since $\rho(y,s)$ is the row
component of $\St_g(y)$.
\item $(y,y',b)$: by (S4), since $(\St_g(y),\St_g(y'),\rho(y,y'))$ is a
reachable pair --- $\rho(y,y')$ is in-pattern or was $f$-generated at
the step where the younger of $y,y'$ was fresh, which is exactly how
the pair fixed point extends (Definition~\ref{def:reach}).
\item $(y,b,b')$: by (S3).
\item $(b,b',b'')$: in-pattern, closed by Definition 3.2.
\end{itemize}
Triples among three ambient occurrences are closed by the invariant
(they were checked at the step where the youngest was fresh).
Atomicity: (S2) excludes $\EQ$.  Safety: (S2).  State and pair
membership for the \emph{next} step is preserved because rows update by
restriction-plus-$f$ and mutual values by $f$ --- the two generators of
the fixed point.  The countable limit is closed since every triple lies
in some finite stage.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Soundness, round-16 form]\label{cor:sound}
If a cluster quasimodel with certified steering exists for $C_0$, then
$C_0$ is satisfiable over abstract complete atomic RCC5 frames with
strong equality.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
As in round 15's Theorem 4.5, with Theorem~\ref{thm:canon} in place of
the withdrawn Lemma 4.3: the truth lemma's universal case now reads the
assigned value's safety directly from (S2)/(S4)-certified data ---
every pair is co-patterned (safe link) or $f$-generated (safe by S2)
--- and the existential case is unchanged (one-step witnesses are
pattern values).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the W1a genus cannot recur here]
The witness needed a pointwise choice whose interaction with a
neighbour's choice was checked nowhere.  In Theorem~\ref{thm:canon}
there are no choices: values are functions of certified states, and
every interaction class is a quantified condition of (Q4$'$) over a
finite reachable set.  WP28 A1 machine-checks that the tenth review's
witness admits \emph{no} steering function (the pair condition with
$W=\PP$ demands $f(\varsigma_Y)(b)\in\Comp(\PP,\DR)=\{\DR\}$, excluded
by (S2)), and that rejection is semantically correct; WP28 B finds
zero unsound accepts and zero closure failures on 208 accepted random
glue steps.
\end{remark}

\section{Extraction and the uniformization obligation (W2$'$)}
\label{sec:extract}

Extraction from a Theorem-A presentation proceeds as in round 15 for
$S$, $\mathcal K$, $\mathcal G$, the core, and width.  What is new is
the steering data: the model supplies, at each gluing, a value
$\rho_{\mathcal M}(y,b)$ \emph{per instance} $y$, while (Q4$'$) demands
a value \emph{per state}.  Extraction therefore requires:

\begin{lemma}[Uniformization; designated W2$'$]\label{lem:unif}
Every satisfiable $C_0$ has a saturated presentation and a state
granularity at which the true relations factor through states: two
ambient occurrences with equal states at a gluing can be assigned equal
fresh rows without loss of satisfiability.
\end{lemma}

\emph{Status of Lemma~\ref{lem:unif}: open; this round's keystone.}
What is known:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Empirically, on 300 random glue steps with selector-free random
safe domains, a state-uniform steering function exists in
$208/211=98.6\%$ of the jointly-realizable instances (WP28 B2).  The
three exceptions are real: joint assignments exist but no state-uniform
one --- two same-state ambient occurrences are forced apart by
different mutual values to third occurrences.
\item Two repair routes for the exceptions, both finite: refine the
state by a bounded summary of the occurrence's realized mutual values
into the reachable pair alphabet (states remain finite; the fixed point
absorbs the refinement); or re-present: Theorem A leaves freedom in
witness selection and bag layout, and the colliding instances can be
separated into different gluings.  Neither route is proved here to
suffice in general; stating the lemma honestly, with its measured
failure rate at the coarsest granularity, is this round's contribution
to it.
\item The direction of risk is completeness (over-rejection), not
soundness: Corollary~\ref{cor:sound} does not depend on
Lemma~\ref{lem:unif}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The (Q2) wording repair}\label{sec:q2}

The tenth review noted (W2c) that round-15 (Q2) quantified over
``interior (non-interface) members'' while delegating via interface
membership --- literally read, delegated fulfilment was disallowed.
Restated: \emph{for every pattern $k$ and every member $a\in V_k$,
every $\exists R.D\in\tau_k(a)$ is fulfilled in $k$ itself, or $a$ is
identified by a gluing of $\mathcal G$ with a member of an adjacent
pattern in which the demand is fulfilled in-pattern.}  No demand
survives two gluings unfulfilled; there are no promissory witnesses.

\section{What would invalidate this delta}\label{sec:invalidate}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(X\arabic*),leftmargin=2.6em]
\item \textbf{Uniformization (Lemma~\ref{lem:unif}) fails structurally}:
a satisfiable concept none of whose presentations admits state-uniform
steering at any bounded refinement --- the keystone, with its failure
rate at the coarsest granularity already measured ($1.4\%$ of random
jointly-realizable steps).
\item \textbf{The reachability fixed point is wrong}: an unfolding
reaching a state or pair outside the computed sets (the suspect
mechanism remains core sharing, W1c), so that (Q4$'$) certifies too
little.
\item \textbf{An error in the four-class triangle taxonomy} of
Theorem~\ref{thm:canon} --- e.g.\ a triple whose youngest member's step
did not have the other two in its reachable pair set.
\item \textbf{The inherited open items}: round-15's W2b dichotomy and
W3 width details, and the two standing external inputs of the stack
(the RCC5 patchwork property; nothing else).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Status}

Ten reviews, ten defects; this delta repairs the tenth and should be
presumed to contain the eleventh.  What has structurally improved: the
soundness half of the decision layer now contains \emph{no
free choices} --- every value is a function of certified finite data,
and every interaction class is a certificate condition; the tenth
review's witness family is provably rejected; and the remaining
mathematical debt is concentrated in one named, empirically measured
lemma (uniformization) plus the untouched width/core details.  The
decidability theorem for $\ALCIRCC$ remains \textbf{strongly supported,
not certified}.
