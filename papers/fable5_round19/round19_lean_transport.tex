\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Round 19 changes the medium.  The certificate transport layer --- the
operational unfolding and the thirteenth review's source-oriented
update U1--U5 --- is now \emph{defined} in Lean~4 (core, no
dependencies): \code{formal/Round19Transport.lean}, 390 lines, one
intentional \code{sorry}.  Totality of the update is
compiler-checked; the canonical order is definitional (a certificate's
attachments are a list); and the thirteenth review's witnesses are
kernel-checked theorems --- including \code{defect12\_written\_gap},
which renders defect \#12 itself as a theorem: round-18's written
case split provably fires no clause on the co-birth pair.  The single
\code{sorry} is the characterization obligation
\code{uSource\_eq\_frame} --- the exact statement that prose failed to
render correctly in rounds 17 and 18, now fixed and unambiguous.  A
mirror harness (WP34) cross-checks a Python transcription of the Lean
definitions against the operational fold on random certificates whose
slot targets range over arbitrary born occurrences (the geometry class
of defects \#11--\#12): 14{,}338 pairs, zero mismatches, deterministic
by construction.  The round's thesis was demonstrated during its own
development: the mirror caught three definitional defects in the Lean
artifact (a padding inconsistency between the update and the row
reader; core-targeting slots duplicating an occurrence inside one
pattern --- now a wellformedness clause; an insufficient fuel bound)
before any reviewer saw them.  Status: \emph{strongly supported, not
certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The single \code{sorry}, and the obligation stack}

\code{uSource\_eq\_frame}: on wellformed certificates, for all distinct
existing occurrences (excluding core--core pairs, which live in the
core net), the operational frame value equals \code{uSource}.  This is
the statement whose prose renditions produced defects \#11 and \#12;
it can no longer drift, because both sides of the equation are the
definitions above.  The adjacent stated obligations: fuel adequacy
(the recursion depth bound $2\cdot\mathrm{steps}+2$, argued in a
comment, to be proved); completeness of the \code{Wellformed}
predicate against Theorem-A extraction; and above this layer, the
round-16 S-conditions, canonical completion, and inclusion theorem ---
the round-20 Lean work.  Below it, the standing completeness
mathematics (F6 width budgets, W2$'$ uniformization) is untouched.

\section{The development log as evidence}

The mirror harness (WP34) computes every pair value twice --- a Python
transcription of \code{uSource} against a Python transcription of the
operational fold --- on random certificates with slot targets ranging
over arbitrary born occurrences, including inherited members of
non-parent copies: exactly where defects \#11 and \#12 lived.  During
development it caught, in order: (i) \code{uSourceFuel} padded
out-of-range slot indices with the value-to-core while the row reader
padded with $DR$ --- a genuine definitional inconsistency, fixed by
aligning the paddings; (ii) slots targeting core occurrences give one
occurrence two ports in one pattern with potentially disagreeing net
columns --- now excluded by the \code{Wellformed} clause (core is
threaded through its dedicated ports, per N3); (iii) the initial fuel
bound $\mathrm{steps}+1$ was insufficient for deep steering chains ---
corrected to $2\cdot\mathrm{steps}+4$ with the depth argument.  In the
prose era each of these would have been a referee finding; in this
round they were caught by the toolchain before review.  Final state:
200 certificates, 663 inherited-member slot targets, 14{,}338 pairs,
zero mismatches; the negative steering control is deterministic (no
salted hash) and fires; the result digest is hash-seed stable.

\section{What would invalidate this round, and status}

(V1) A wellformed certificate on which \code{uSource\_eq\_frame} fails
--- WP34 searched the inherited-member class without finding one, and
any such example would now be a concrete Lean disproof target rather
than a prose dispute.  (V2) An extraction-side need for certificate
shapes outside \code{Wellformed} (core-targeting slots, non-injective
maps) --- each exclusion is documented with its failure mode.  (V3)
The standing completeness items F6 and W2$'$, and the single external
input (the RCC5 patchwork property).  Ledger: thirteen reviews ---
twelve defects with witnesses, one no-counterexample.  This round is
unreviewed; its natural review is different in kind: attack the Lean
statements, or prove the \code{sorry}.  The decidability theorem for
$\ALCIRCC$ remains \textbf{strongly supported, not certified}.
