# Related work and conservative novelty assessment

## Bottom line

No inspected source proves the exact no-universal-PO theorem or uses the complete
combination of lower-spectrum cone signatures, RCC5-specific compatibility,
fresh ordered-disjoint unfolding, protected residual PO, and a source-model
post-fixed-point completeness proof.

The standard ingredients are not new: closure/types, monotone GFP elimination,
fresh-witness unravelling, RCC5 composition, and order with hereditary conflict.
The result and their RCC5-specific coupling appear new relative to the direct corpus
checked. This is a provisional priority assessment, not a categorical first-ever claim.

## Closest direct sources

- Cohn (1993), "Modal and non modal qualitative spatial logics": early RCC-as-modalities source.
- Wessel (2000-2003), especially Technical Report FBI-HH-M-324/03: direct
  `ALCI_RCC` problem family and unanswered RCC5/RCC8 cases.
- Lutz and Wolter (2006), "Modal logics of topological relations," LMCS 2(2:2):
  closest peer-reviewed modal comparator; no no-universal-PO cone theorem located.

The artifact's abstract composition-table semantics should not be identified with
Lutz-Wolter region-structure semantics until the packaged adequacy bridge is proved.

## Important neighboring formalism

Haarslev, Lutz, and Moeller (1999), "A Description Logic with Concrete Domains and
a Role-Forming Predicate Operator," can derive roles from concrete predicates.
Therefore the broad claim "concrete-domain logics cannot quantify over spatial
relations" is false without qualification. Their feature-mediated, two-sorted system
and safety restrictions differ from the artifact's primitive single-sorted total RCC5
partition, but a definition-level subsumption/separation audit remains necessary.

## Structural analogy

After reversing PP orientation, the ordered-disjoint normal form is the relational
skeleton of a prime event structure: causality, hereditary conflict, and residual
concurrency. See Nielsen-Plotkin-Winskel (1981) and Winskel (1987). The event-structure
geometry is prior art; its RCC5 modal labelling, cone spectra, and decision proof are
the specific contribution here.
