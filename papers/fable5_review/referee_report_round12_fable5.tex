\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report reviews the canonical round-12 decidability manuscript
\code{papers/automata\_route\_repairs/split\_forest\_automata\_with\_appendices.tex}
(29\,pp, May 31, 2026) and attacks it with three new computational probes
(WP14--WP16, under \code{verification/python/}).  Summary verdict:
\textbf{genuine gap; the theorem remains most likely true; the repair is
localized and computationally corroborated.}  The round-12 architecture is a
real improvement --- the route-dependent transformer layer is gone, and
global RCC5 coherence of the \emph{bag networks} genuinely follows from the
RCC5 patchwork property plus a correct compactness argument (WP14 verifies
the patchwork statement exhaustively at small widths: $63{,}219$
amalgamation instances, zero failures).  But the Truth Lemma of Theorem~B(b)
contains one unproved load-bearing step: it assumes the declared
relation-vector \emph{shadows} agree with the patchwork completion
$\rho_T$, although $\rho_T$ is amalgamated from bag networks \emph{only} and
no listed legality test constrains two shadows against each other.  WP15
exhibits a three-bag configuration that passes every listed test yet admits
\emph{no} frame realizing all declared vectors (23 of 64 assignments on the
minimal pattern fail); the missing statement is a \emph{Shadow Amalgamation
Lemma}, the round-12 successor of the sixth review's transformer-coherence
gap C1 --- same genus, one layer up, but now much smaller.  Two independent
specification loopholes (EQ-valued shadow entries; undefined shadow scope)
are each shown by WP16 to flip the referee concept $C_{\mathrm{split}}$ and
three other unsatisfiable forced-composition concepts to ACCEPT; both have
one-line fixes.  Under the repaired discipline, WP16's mechanism agrees
with the cover-tree tableau on all ten diagnostics --- including the
strong-EQ PO-loop concept that the retired quasimodel reasoner decided
wrongly --- and on a 148-concept random sweep with zero disagreements.  No
accepted-but-unsatisfiable \emph{concept} was found.  Status
recommendation: keep ``strongly supported, \emph{not} certified''; the
keystone is now the Shadow Amalgamation Lemma plus the $K(C_0)$
finiteness accounting.
\end{abstract}

\section{Summary verdict}\label{sec:verdict}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{The architectural move of round-12 is correct and real
progress.}  Deleting the pair/triple transformer layer and resting global
RCC5 coherence on complete atomic bag networks + the patchwork property
removes the sixth review's C1 \emph{for the bag layer}: Appendix~A's finite
tree-patching induction is sound (its use of running intersection is
exactly right), the compactness passage to countable trees is correct
(modulo a wording slip, Finding~\ref{f:appA}), and the two-bag patchwork
property itself --- the one algebraic input --- holds in exactly the stated
form for the abstract semantics, now machine-verified exhaustively at small
widths (WP14, $63{,}219$ instances, zero failures).

\item \textbf{The Truth Lemma of Theorem B(b) has one unproved load-bearing
step (Finding~\ref{f:shadow}, critical).}  $\rho_T$ is amalgamated from
\emph{bag networks only} (App.~E, ``Global realization''), yet the universal
case asserts ``the exact relation vector from $x$ to the live ports includes
$\rho_T(x,y)=R$''.  Nothing forces the declared shadow vectors to agree with
$\rho_T$: no listed test relates two shadows to each other, and a vector
entry to a port is dropped when the port is forgotten.  WP15 gives a
three-bag configuration passing \emph{every} listed test whose declared
vectors are \emph{jointly unrealizable} --- the quoted sentence is false for
every choice of $\rho_T$ on that input.  The missing statement is a
\emph{Shadow Amalgamation Lemma}.  This is the same \emph{genus} of defect
as the sixth review's C1 (local checks that do not compose globally), one
layer up (shadow tier instead of transformer tier) --- but the layer is far
thinner, the failure is now precisely isolated, and two repair disciplines
are computationally corroborated (Section~\ref{sec:repair}).

\item \textbf{Two specification loopholes are independently
soundness-fatal as literally written} (Findings~\ref{f:eq}
and~\ref{f:scope}); each admits a one-line fix, and WP16 machine-checks
that with the fixes in place the mechanism decides all referee diagnostics
correctly, while either loophole alone flips $C_{\mathrm{split}}$,
$C_{\mathrm{force}}$ and two deeper forced-composition concepts to ACCEPT.

\item \textbf{The $K(C_0)$ finiteness argument is still an assertion}
(Finding~\ref{f:kbound}) --- the sixth review's M1 in softened form.

\item \textbf{No accepted-but-unsatisfiable concept was found.}  Like the
sixth reviewer, I could not exploit the gaps at \emph{concept} level:
$\mathcal{ALCI}$ lacks nominals, so the witness-identification needed to
force two distinct occurrences into my WP15 configuration appears
inexpressible.  The configuration-level defect stands regardless: the Truth
Lemma, as proved, is false.

\item \textbf{Status recommendation.}  Keep ``strongly supported,
\emph{not} certified.''  Do not describe round-12 as cold-reviewed.  The
keystone has narrowed again: from ``patchwork-bag adequacy'' generically to
\emph{Shadow Amalgamation} + \emph{$K(C_0)$ accounting} specifically.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Findings}\label{sec:findings}

Throughout, $\Comp$ is the RCC5 composition table (re-verified, S1), and
``the listed tests'' means the union of Definition~3.4 (V1)--(V8), the
Appendix~H shadow-movement conditions (boundary agreement on shared ports;
compatibility with the current bag network), and the Appendix~Q tests
(N1)--(N6), (T1)--(T4), (W1)--(W3), (O1)--(O5), (C1)--(C3), (S1)--(S4).

\begin{finding}[\textbf{CRITICAL --- the Truth Lemma assumes an unproved
Shadow Amalgamation Lemma}]\label{f:shadow}
\emph{Location:} Theorem~3.6(b) proof; Appendix~E (``Global realization'',
``Truth lemma''); Appendix~M (``Truth lemma: universal cases'',
``Truth lemma: existential cases'').

\emph{The step.}  The realization builds $\rho_T$ by ``the countable
patching lemma \ldots{} applied to the tree-shaped family of finite
\emph{bag networks}'' --- shadow vectors are not part of the amalgamated
family.  The universal case then argues: when target $y$ is introduced,
``the exact relation vector from $x$ to the live ports includes
$\rho_T(x,y)=R$; validity therefore forces $D$ into the type of $y$.''
The existential case for vertical requests likewise fulfills
$\exists R.D$ at ``a port $q$ whose exact recorded relation to the source
is $R$'' --- where, if the source is no longer live, ``recorded'' can only
mean the shadow entry.

\emph{Why it does not follow.}  For a pair $(x,y)$ that is never co-bagged,
$\rho_T(x,y)$ is whatever the bag-only amalgam happens to choose among the
(generally several) patchwork-feasible values, while the declared vector
entry is a separate guess constrained only \emph{per bag} (one-point
compatibility) and \emph{per boundary} (rigidity on continued ports).
Two gaps result:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=2em]
\item nothing makes $\rho_T$ agree with the declared entries; and
\item one cannot repair this by amalgamating the entries too, because the
listed tests never constrain two \emph{shadows} against each other, nor a
vector entry declared while a port was live against entries declared after
that port is forgotten.  The implicit lemma ---
\emph{bags $+$ all declared shadow rows are jointly realizable in one
frame} --- is false for the tests as written.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Machine-checked counterexample} (WP15, part P1; three bags on a
decomposition path, one port each, separators empty --- a variant with a
continued all-$\DR$ separator port behaves identically):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
bag & live port & declared shadow entries at this bag\\
\midrule
$t_0$ & $x_1$ & --- ($x_1$ introduced, then forgotten)\\
$t_1$ & $x_2$ & $x_1$: $(x_2\mapsto\DR)$\\
$t_2$ & $p$   & $x_1$: $(p\mapsto\PP)$;\quad $x_2$: $(p\mapsto\PPI)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every bag is (trivially) a complete atomic closed network; every row is
one-point composition-closed with its bag; rigidity is vacuous
(no continued ports); all entries are non-$\EQ$; the $(x_1,x_2)$ entry even
respects the residual-frontier discipline ($\DR$).  Yet jointly the three
declarations say $x_1\,\PP\,p$, $p\,\PP\,x_2$ (converse of the declared
$\PPI$), hence $\rho(x_1,x_2)\in\Comp(\PP,\PP)=\{\PP\}$, contradicting the
declared $\DR$.  \textbf{No abstract RCC5 frame realizes bags $+$ rows;}
consequently, for every choice of $\rho_T$, at least one declared vector
entry is wrong, and the Truth Lemma's quoted sentence fails: an obligation
$(\rho_T(x_1,x_2),D)\in\Omega_{x_1}$ can fire in the realized frame on a
target whose type was never checked for $D$ (the automaton checked the
$\DR$ entry instead).  WP15's exhaustive sweep of the pattern shows the
defect is generic: \textbf{23 of the 64} legal row assignments are jointly
unrealizable.

\emph{What survives.}  With a \emph{single} shadow the implicit lemma is
provable and empirically clean (WP15 P3: 250/250 realizable): the extended
family $\{N_t\cup\text{row}_t\}$ is itself a tree-shaped agreeing family of
complete atomic networks (the source becomes a member of every bag in its
connected scope; rigidity is exactly overlap agreement), so
Theorem~2.5 applies to it verbatim.  The defect is strictly about
\emph{multiple} shadows and \emph{dropped} entries.  This localizes the
missing lemma sharply; see Section~\ref{sec:repair}.

\emph{Relation to prior reviews.}  This is the successor of the sixth
review's C1: round-12 removed the route-dependent transformer layer, but
the shadow layer still carries cross-bag relational commitments whose
global coherence is asserted rather than derived from patchwork.  The
manuscript's own Appendix~U names the failure mode (F3: ``if relation-vector
shadows failed to preserve universal obligations across bag boundaries, the
automaton could accept false abstractions'').
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{MAJOR (spec, soundness-fatal as written, one-line
fix) --- $\EQ$-valued shadow entries are not excluded}]\label{f:eq}
\emph{Location:} Definition~3.3 ($\vec r\in\Rel^P$ with
$\Rel=\{\EQ,\PP,\PPI,\PO,\DR\}$); Appendix~Q (no test excludes $\EQ$
entries; the no-$\EQ$ tests (N3)/(N4) apply to bag networks only).

A shadow whose entry to a target port is declared $\EQ$ triggers \emph{no}
obligation $(R,D)$ with $R\neq\EQ$, while under strong equality the realized
relation between the two distinct occurrences is necessarily non-$\EQ$.  On
the referee concept
$C_{\mathrm{split}}=\exists\PP.(A\sqcap\lnot B\sqcap\forall\PP.\lnot
B\sqcap\forall\PPI.\lnot B\sqcap\forall\PO.\lnot B)\sqcap\exists\PP.B$
the $y$-shadow may declare its entry to $z$ as $\EQ$; all three universal
obligations then stay silent, and the abstraction is accepted although
$\Comp(\PPI,\PP)=\{\EQ,\PP,\PPI,\PO\}$ minus $\EQ$ leaves only
obligation-violating options.  WP16 (literal mode) machine-checks the
acceptance.  \emph{Fix:} require shadow entries to range over
$\Rel\setminus\{\EQ\}$ (an entry $\EQ$ is legal only as the statement that
the source \emph{is} that port's occurrence, i.e.\ never for a genuine
shadow).
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{MAJOR (spec, soundness-fatal as written) --- the
shadow's scope is never pinned down}]\label{f:scope}
\emph{Location:} Appendix~H (``universally branches to all adjacent bags in
which the shadow \emph{must} continue'' --- ``must'' is not defined);
Definition~3.4 (V5) (``every introduced target is checked'' --- by which
shadow, where?); Appendix~S.

If a run may simply not carry the shadow of $x$ into the subtree where $y$
lives, the pair $(x,y)$ escapes all checks; the completeness direction never
objects (a real model needs no check), so the choice is unconstrained.
WP16 (literal mode) shows the consequence: the unsatisfiable
$C_{\mathrm{force}}$, $C_{\mathrm{split}}$,
$C_{\mathrm{2hop}}=\exists\DR.(\exists\PPI.A)\sqcap\forall\DR.\lnot A$ and
$C_{\mathrm{3hop}}=\exists\DR.(\exists\PPI.\exists\PPI.A)\sqcap
\forall\DR.\lnot A$ are all ACCEPTED when distant pairs escape obligations.
Note that mandatory propagation cannot be depth-bounded:
$\Comp(\DR,\PPI)=\{\DR\}$ forces a $\DR$-obligation into \emph{every}
proper part of a $\DR$-witness, arbitrarily deep, so obligation-carrying
shadows must be propagated into entire subtrees.  That is unproblematic for
an alternating automaton (spawn a copy per direction forever; the state
space is the finite set of profiles) --- but it must be \emph{required} by
the transition function, not left to the run.  \emph{Fix:} make shadow
propagation mandatory: for every source with a nonempty obligation set, a
shadow copy is spawned into every child direction and to the parent, at
every bag of the run.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{MAJOR --- $K(C_0)$ finiteness is still asserted
(sixth review M1, softened)}]\label{f:kbound}
\emph{Location:} Lemma~3.5 (\code{lem:K-bound}) and Appendix~K (K1)--(K4).

The rank-0 step says: ``one enumerates finite labelled contexts, keeps
those satisfying local RCC5 consistency and saturation, and quotients by
isomorphism\ldots{}  This is finite.''  As written it is not: the
saturation rules (S1)--(S6) are conditional-insert closure rules, so a
context with arbitrarily many mutually-$\DR$ ports (all conditions
vacuously satisfied) passes them at every size, and isomorphism classes
grow without bound.  The enumeration in Appendix~K terminates only if
saturated contexts \emph{needed for $C_0$} have bounded minimal
representatives --- which is precisely the claim to be proved.  The round-11
recurrence was faulted for over-claiming a specific bound (M1); round-12
responds by claiming no bound at all, which under-delivers: \emph{some}
explicit accounting is needed.  \emph{Fix:} define side contexts as
\emph{witness-generated} (every port carries a generation reason: a
selected witness of a closure formula at a specific source, a boundary
insertion under (S2), a stratum/threshold node under (S3)/(S4), or a
recursive-context port under (S5)); then bound reasons per source by
$|\Cl(C_0)|$ plus the boundary size and prove the closure operator stays
within a computable per-rank recurrence --- or sidestep entirely with the
$2^{\mathrm{poly}(|\Cl(C_0)|)}$ type/mosaic bound the sixth review
recommended.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{MINOR --- Appendix A countable-patching wording}]
\label{f:appA}
The closed sets are defined as labellings ``extending \emph{the} patchwork
completion on the occurrences occurring in $S$'' --- there is no canonical
completion, and with the definite article the finite-intersection argument
breaks (different subtrees would pin incompatible completions).  Replace by
``whose restriction to the occurrences of $S$ is \emph{some} complete
atomic composition-closed extension of all bags in $S$''.  With that
reading the argument is correct (checked in detail): the sets are clopen
cylinders, a common finite super-subtree witnesses the finite intersection
property (restriction of a completion of the larger subtree to a smaller
one remains a completion because (R1)--(R3) are universal conditions),
and every pair/triple of occurrences lies in some finite connected subtree
by trace-connectedness, so the limit labelling satisfies all constraints.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{MINOR --- parity narrative and input-tree
formalities}]\label{f:parity}
(i) The ``generalized B\"uchi via round-robin counter'' construction is the
single-thread device; in an \emph{alternating} automaton each spawned
request copy can simply carry a rejecting (odd) priority until it enters
its fulfilled state, which is simpler and avoids any inter-request
bookkeeping.  Either is fine; the text should commit to one and state the
priority assignment.  (ii) ``Runs on ranked trees over $\Sigma_{C_0}$'':
bag labels do not determine an arity; say explicitly that trees are padded
to a fixed maximal branching degree (computable from $K$ and $|\Cl(C_0)|$)
with a dummy label.  (iii) Definition~3.4 (V8) ties a vertical request to
``the same overlap-equivalence class of the source occurrence''; if this is
meant to keep the source \emph{live} (co-bagged) along its support branch
--- which makes request fulfillment a bag-network fact and immunizes it
against Finding~\ref{f:shadow} --- say so explicitly; it is the right
choice.
\end{finding}

\begin{finding}[\textbf{OBSERVATION --- the parity/request-closure content
is load-bearing (incidental replication of the round-6/7 defect)}]
\label{f:blocking}
While building WP16's reference machine, a first implementation used naive
same-type blocking for vertical eventualities.  It promptly produced a
false SAT on $C_{\mathrm{3hop}}$ by deferring the eventuality
$\exists\PPI.A$ forever down the regular tail --- precisely the lap-collapse
family of defects from the no-automata thread (rounds 6/7), and precisely
what a parity condition must reject.  The fix (a lap may block only if the
blocking occurrence fulfilled the \emph{same} request concretely) restored
agreement with the tableau.  This is independent corroboration that
Theorem~C's request states must enforce fulfillment \emph{on every lap},
and that any implementation gets this wrong by default.
\end{finding}

\section{Steps attacked and found sound}\label{sec:sound}

\begin{soundstep}[Composition table]
Re-derived independently from set semantics over a 5-element universe
(WP14 T0); matches the manuscript's table in all 25 cells.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[The patchwork theorem as stated (Theorem 2.5)]
For the abstract composition-table semantics, ``satisfiable finite complete
atomic network'' $=$ ``composition-closed network'', so the two-bag
patchwork property is a purely combinatorial statement.  WP14 verifies it
\textbf{exhaustively} for one fresh variable per side with shared parts of
size $0..3$ ($21{,}538$ agreeing pairs) and for two fresh variables per side
with shared parts of size $1..2$ ($41{,}681$ pairs, sampled at the largest
class), and verifies the finite tree-patching lemma on 300 random
tree-shaped bag families (3--6 bags, width $\le 7$) by literally executing
Appendix~A's leaf-by-leaf induction.  \textbf{Zero failures.}  This turns
the proof's central external dependency into a machine-checked fact at
small width (complementing WP6), in \emph{exactly} the form the manuscript
uses it.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Finite tree patching's use of running intersection]
The induction step needs the leaf to agree with the \emph{remainder} on
their full shared variable set; connected traces give
$V_L\cap V_{\mathrm{rest}} = V_L\cap V_M$ for the unique neighbor $M$,
which is exactly what the proof uses.  Correct.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Strong equality / Appendix G]
Correct given atomic completions: every completion in the amalgam is atomic
with $\EQ$ only on the diagonal, and no cell of the composition table can
force a cross pair to be exactly $\EQ$ --- WP14 T4 verifies that every
nontrivial cell containing $\EQ$ also contains $\PP,\PPI,\PO$.  Together
with exact occurrence identity this cleanly eliminates the historical
blocking-vs-equality confusion (Appendix~R's trace-connectedness argument
is also correct).
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[The strong-EQ loop concepts]
The mechanism (exact occurrence identity + same-bag witness reuse) decides
the PO-loop concept
$C\sqcap\exists\PO.\exists\PO.C\sqcap\forall\{\PO,\DR,\PP,\PPI\}.\lnot C$
correctly as SAT (WP16), the known blind spot of the retired quasimodel
reasoner.  The universal constraints force the second-hop witness to be the
root occurrence itself, and reuse expresses exactly that.  This is an
architectural strength of round-12.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[$C_{\mathrm{split}}$ under the repaired discipline]
With Findings \ref{f:eq}/\ref{f:scope} fixed, the rejection mechanism works
as Appendix~O claims: the shared source $x$ keeps $y,z$ connected through
its overlap class; the $y$-shadow's entry to $z$ is triangle-constrained
through $x$ to $\Comp(\PPI,\PP)\setminus\{\EQ\}=\{\PP,\PPI,\PO\}$, and each
option violates one of $y$'s universals against $z{:}B$.  WP16 confirms
REJECT in repaired mode, ACCEPT under either loophole.  The sixth review's
C2 is thus \emph{addressed conditional on} Findings
\ref{f:shadow}--\ref{f:scope}.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Forest-to-tree packaging (sixth review C3.3)]
Dissolved by design: the decomposition tree is a data structure, not the
containment order; vertical direction is a per-edge annotation, upward
growth is a child bag, and the cross-tree links of the semantic forest are
side-context bags.  No residual objection.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Two-wayness and emptiness]
Choosing a two-way alternating parity tree automaton and citing emptiness
(Vardi) is the right resolution of the sixth review's M3; the lossless
one-way summary burden is genuinely gone.  The state families are finite
given the profile alphabet.  (Presentational points in
Finding~\ref{f:parity}.)
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Witness thinning; types; normalization]
Lemma 3.1's complement-closure argument is correct and standard (as in
prior reviews); the $\EQ$-normalization and Hintikka machinery are correct.
\end{soundstep}

\begin{soundstep}[Random cross-validation of the repaired mechanism]
WP16's repaired-discipline machine agrees with the cover-tree tableau on
148/148 decided random concepts (2 skipped at budget), in addition to all
ten diagnostics.  No adequacy anomaly surfaced.
\end{soundstep}

\section{The repair program}\label{sec:repair}

The missing keystone is now one lemma.  Two routes, both compatible with
the manuscript's architecture; route (a) is recommended.

\subsection*{(a) The discipline route (recommended)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(R\arabic*),leftmargin=2.6em]
\item \textbf{Vertical liveness.}  Extend saturation/(V7) to the shadow
layer: a pair whose relation is (or becomes, by forced composition)
$\PP$, $\PPI$ or $\EQ$ must be \emph{co-bagged} (live-live), never
shadow-carried.  Since the only compositions forcing a vertical atom are
$\PP;\PP=\{\PP\}$, $\PPI;\PPI=\{\PPI\}$ (and $\EQ$-trivial ones), forced
verticals arise only along containment chains, which the presentation
already keeps live; the WP15 counterexample (which needs $\PP$/$\PPI$
shadow entries) becomes ill-formed.  Shadow entries then range over
$\{\DR,\PO\}$.
\item \textbf{Horizontal Shadow Amalgamation Lemma} (to be proved): bag
networks + $\DR/\PO$-only shadow rows satisfying one-point closure and
rigidity are jointly realizable.  Proof strategy: fix each shadow's
extended family (sound alone, by the single-shadow argument / WP15 P3);
complete shadow-shadow pairs by $\DR$ --- every cell
$\Comp(r,s^{-1})$ with $r,s\in\{\DR,\PO\}$ contains $\DR$ --- and derive
triangle-closure through ports from the per-bag one-point closure plus
directional propagation along separators.  WP15 P4 found no counterexample
(358 configurations).
\item \textbf{Bookkeeping fixes.}  Findings \ref{f:eq}, \ref{f:scope},
\ref{f:parity}(iii): non-$\EQ$ entries; mandatory propagation; requests
keep their source live along the support branch.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{(b) The extended-bag route}
Promote in-scope shadows to \emph{virtual ports}: each bag carries a
complete atomic closed network on $P\cup\mathrm{Sh}$ \emph{including
shadow-shadow entries}, with boundary agreement on continued shadows.  The
family is then literally a tree-shaped agreeing family and Theorem~2.5
applies verbatim (WP15 P5: no failures).  The cost is real: along an
infinite branch the number of simultaneously in-scope shadows is unbounded,
so the \emph{alphabet} must quotient shadow instances by profile, and the
quotient needs its own lemma --- same-profile instances must be pairwise
realizable ($\PP$ and $\PO$ are always available in
$\Comp(r,r^{-1})$, but a profile whose own obligations forbid all four
non-$\EQ$ relations to its own type --- e.g.\ the PO-loop type --- is
\emph{solitary} and must not be duplicated).  This route is doable but
heavier than (a).
