TARGET G3 -- THE COVERAGE WALK (round-13 Lemma 6.1, item G3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Claim to attack/prove: in a valid D-abstraction, for every occurrence x
with nonempty obligation set and every occurrence y, the pair (x,y) is
(a) co-bagged, (b) row-covered, or (c) rho_T(x,y) in {PP,PPI} realized
along a co-bagged uniformly-oriented vertical chain (through which D3
propagates). Scrutinize specifically:

  G3a The walk induction: crossings either keep horizontal entries or
      verticalize; is the case split exhaustive? What exactly happens on
      MIXED walks (horizontal crossings interleaved with
      verticalizations)? The manuscript dismisses them in one sentence
      ("end in cases (a)/(b) at the last horizontal manifestation's bag
      family") -- verify or refute with a concrete tree-of-bags
      configuration.
  G3b Orientation bookkeeping in case (c): the chain composes to exactly
      R by Comp(PP,PP)={PP} -- but must the chain be uniformly oriented?
      Can rho_T(x,y) be vertical while every connecting chain mixes PP
      and PPI edges (Comp(PP,PPI) = all five)? If yes, D3 does not reach
      y and the truth lemma's case (iii) has a hole.
  G3c The interaction with Theorem 4.3: for obligation-carrying x, can
      the amalgam assign a vertical value to a pair that is neither
      co-bagged nor chain-connected? (The intended argument: D2 makes x
      row-cover every port, and row values are horizontal and realized
      verbatim -- so vertical rho_T values can only occur where rows are
      absent, i.e. co-bagged or chain cases. Check this reasoning against
      the definition of "row-covered" and the verticalization fallback:
      after a fallback, is the NEW manifestation's row coverage complete
      in all directions, including backwards?)
