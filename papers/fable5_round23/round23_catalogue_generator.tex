\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Round-22 lifted the S-condition to catalogue data
(\code{SCat}) but still required, per certificate, that it be
\emph{wellformed} and \emph{faithful}.  Round-23 discharges both by
construction.  \code{formal/Round19Transport.lean} (4{,}056 lines,
still \textbf{zero} \code{sorry}) now defines a \code{Catalog} ---
templates, N2 attachment rules (each child slot maps to a parent
member port, never core), a root, and template-indexed steering ---
and a builder \code{buildCert} that turns a \emph{plan} (a list of
rule/parent choices) into a certificate.  The round-23 theorems prove
that for any structurally sound catalogue (\code{CatOk}) and any valid
plan (\code{PlanOk}), the built certificate is \code{Wellformed}
(\code{build\_wellformed}) and \code{Faithful} (\code{build\_faithful})
--- so \code{catalogue\_soundness} derives the full per-unfolding
\code{SCond} for \emph{every} plan from the single catalogue check
\code{SCat}.  Faithfulness --- the thirteenth review's parent-pattern
agreement, the last per-certificate hypothesis --- is proved by a
parent-chain induction from each rule's port-agreement condition.
``Every unfolding of the catalogue'' is thereby a theorem about a
syntactic object rather than a hypothesis pair.  A concrete catalogue
witness (\code{certK}) drives the entire rounds 19--23 pipeline to a
closed frame with no per-certificate proof.  Status: \emph{strongly
supported, not certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \code{Catalog}, \code{Attach} (child template, expected parent
template, N2 slot map), and \code{buildCert}: a plan folds attachment
rules onto the root, each child inheriting its parent's member
occurrences through the slot map.  Indexing lemmas
(\code{buildSteps\_length}, \code{buildCert\_step\_zero},
\code{buildCert\_step\_succ}) pin what each built step is.
\item \code{CatOk} / \code{AttachOk} / \code{PlanOk}: structural
soundness of the catalogue, of each rule (valid templates, injective
slot map into parent member ports, and \emph{port agreement} --- old
child ports and their parent images carry equal net values), and of a
plan (real rules, earlier parents, matching parent templates).
\item \code{build\_wellformed}: every planned certificate satisfies
all seven \code{Wellformed} clauses --- targets pre-exist, are born
and in range, one per slot, injective; templates valid; nets
converse-coherent; steering reads only rows.  Proved by induction over
the step prefix; slot injectivity descends from the rule's
\code{map\_nodup} through the port map.
\item \code{build\_faithful}: co-members of a built step carry the
step template's net entry.  Proved by a \emph{parent-chain
induction}: each old child port maps to a parent member port
(\code{built\_port\_parent}), the parent pair value is the parent
template's entry by the inductive hypothesis, and the rule's
\code{agree} condition transports it to the child template --- exactly
the thirteenth review's parent-pattern agreement, now structural.
\item \code{catalogue\_soundness} (ROUND-23 MAIN THEOREM) and
\code{catalogue\_frame\_closed}: \code{CatOk} + \code{PlanOk} +
\code{SCat} give \code{SCond}, hence closed frames, for every plan.
\item Witness \code{certK}: a root template (two fresh ports) and one
rule whose slot inherits a root fresh port.
\code{certK\_catok} / \code{certK\_planok} / \code{certK\_scat} drive
\code{certK\_scond} and a closed-frame \code{example} through
\code{buildCert}, with no per-certificate wellformedness or
faithfulness proof written.
\end{itemize}

\section{What remains}

With round-23 the soundness pipeline is complete at the relational
level: from catalogue syntax to closed atomic frames, every step
kernel-checked.  The remaining soundness work is the logic layer
(round-24: $\ALCIRCC$ syntax and semantics over frames, Hintikka
types, one-step demand fulfilment, the truth lemma) and the limit
passage (round-25: finite prefixes to the full presentation, absorbing
the RCC5 patchwork property).  The completeness direction (extraction
from Theorem-A presentations) still contains the open mathematics F6
(width budgets) and W2$'$ (uniformization).  Ledger: thirteen reviews
--- twelve defects, one no-counterexample; rounds 19--23 unreviewed.
The decidability theorem for $\ALCIRCC$ remains \textbf{strongly
supported, not certified}.
