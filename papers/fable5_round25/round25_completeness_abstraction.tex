\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Round-24 proved the soundness direction: a certificate carrying a
Hintikka labelling yields a model.  Round-25 proves the abstraction is
also \emph{complete}: every model induces a Hintikka labelling, so
satisfiability and Hintikka-realizability \emph{coincide}
(\code{satisfiable\_iff\_hintikkaP}).  \code{formal/Round19Transport.lean}
(4{,}522 lines, still \textbf{zero} \code{sorry}) now proves this
biconditional --- the \emph{completeness of the type-system
abstraction}: the certificate loses nothing relative to models.  The
completeness proof is short because the Hintikka clauses are literally
the recursion clauses of satisfaction (\code{model\_hintikkaP}).  The
certificate/generation interface is also proved
(\code{generated\_satisfiable}), and a concrete two-point model
witnesses a \emph{real fulfilled existential} ($A_0 \sqcap
\exists\mathrm{DR}.A_1$) --- the one-step demand discharge that the
no-automata thread never achieved by hand.  Crucially, this round
\emph{honestly delimits what is open}: \code{CompletenessObligation}
states --- but does \emph{not} prove and does \emph{not} axiomatize ---
the one remaining target, that every satisfiable concept admits a
\emph{finite} catalogue and labelling.  Its two sub-parts are the
project's long-standing open items F6 (width budgets) and W2$'$
(uniformization).  Status: \emph{strongly supported, not certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \code{HintikkaP} / \code{truth\_lemmaP}: the Hintikka conditions
and truth lemma in predicate form (labellings need not be a
certificate's occurrence lists).
\item \code{model\_hintikkaP}: every interpretation's own satisfaction
relation $\{(x,C) : x \models C\}$ is a Hintikka labelling.  This is
the completeness half, and it is nearly immediate --- the Hintikka
clauses are exactly the recursion clauses of \code{sat}.
\item \code{satisfiable\_iff\_hintikkaP}: $C_0$ is satisfiable
\emph{iff} there is an RCC5 frame with a Hintikka labelling putting
$C_0$ at a domain point.  The two directions are
\code{truth\_lemmaP} (soundness) and \code{model\_hintikkaP}
(completeness).  So the type-system certificate is a faithful
abstraction of models.
\item \code{hintikka\_listP}: a round-24 List-labelling induces a
predicate labelling --- certificates plug into the abstract framework.
\item \code{generated\_satisfiable}: the completeness INTERFACE --- a
structurally sound catalogue with a valid plan, the catalogue check,
and a root-anchored Hintikka labelling yields a model (composing
rounds 23--24).
\item \code{sample\_satisfiable\_ex}: $A_0 \sqcap \exists\mathrm{DR}.A_1$
is satisfiable in a two-point DR model.  The root's existential is met
by an actual DR-neighbour carrying $A_1$ --- one step, no promissory
witness, with composition closure holding
($\mathrm{comp}(\mathrm{DR},\mathrm{DR}) \ni \mathrm{DR},\mathrm{EQ}$).
\end{itemize}

\section{What is open --- stated precisely}

\code{CompletenessObligation} is a Lean \code{def} (a proposition), not
a theorem and not an axiom: \emph{for every satisfiable $C_0$ there
exist a catalogue $K$, a valid plan, a Hintikka labelling $\tau$ and a
root with $C_0 \in \tau(\text{root})$, all finite}.  With this
obligation, \code{satisfiable\_iff\_hintikkaP} and
\code{generated\_satisfiable} close the completeness direction, and the
finite type space (at most $2^{|\mathrm{closure}(C_0)|}$ types) turns
the search into a decision procedure.  The obligation's two hard
sub-parts are exactly the project's standing open items:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{F6} --- the $K(C_0)$ width budget: bounding the number
of live occurrences per cluster so the catalogue is finite and the
generation terminates.
\item \textbf{W2$'$} --- uniformization: that model values factor
through finitely many interface states (measured $98.6\%$ in WP28; two
finite repair routes sketched; soundness does not depend on it, so the
risk is over-rejection only).
\end{itemize}
These are genuine mathematics, not transcription; no round count is
promised for them.  What round-25 establishes is that \emph{nothing
else} stands between the current kernel-checked development and
decidability --- the entire soundness pipeline and the completeness of
the abstraction are done; only the finite realizability of a
satisfiable concept remains.
