\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Round-21's \code{SCond} quantifies over the occurrences of one
unfolding.  Round-22 lifts the check to the catalogue:
\code{formal/Round19Transport.lean} (3{,}225 lines, still
\textbf{zero} \code{sorry}) now defines \code{SCat} --- the
S-condition stated on \code{(coreNet, templates, f)} \emph{alone},
with enumerated abstract rows in place of occurrences, so the check
is independent of the step count --- and proves \code{scat\_scond}:
\code{SCat} plus pattern faithfulness (\code{Faithful}, the
thirteenth review's parent-pattern agreement as a per-certificate
condition) imply the full per-unfolding \code{SCond}.  One finite
check thus certifies unboundedly many step lists, and all round-21
frame theorems apply downstream (\code{scat\_frame\_closed}).  The
mathematical heart is the steered-steered case: the manuscripts'
joint steering (round-16 Q4$'$) made exact --- the old pair's value
enters the abstract check \emph{constrained through every separator
member} by closure below the current step, discharged by a
strengthened induction (\code{fresh\_tri} +
\code{closedBelow\_all}).  Both poles are kernel-checked:
\code{certC} passes the catalogue check and recovers its
\code{SCond} through the abstract route, while \code{certD}'s
catalogue check provably fails at exactly the reachable row
($\mathrm{DR}$ against $\comp(\mathrm{PPI},\mathrm{PO})$).  Status:
\emph{strongly supported, not certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \code{SCat}: twelve fields on catalogue data only --- core net
conditions (converse coherence, properness, closure, N3 row
agreement), per-template composition closure and properness, and six
steering conditions quantified over \emph{enumerated} abstract rows
(\code{atomLists}): member/steered in both orientations, same-source
two-fresh in both orientations, the joint steered-steered condition,
and steering properness.  The step index appears only to select which
template/steering pair is in force; for template-determined steering
--- the round-23 generator's discipline --- the conditions collapse
to one check per template.
\item \code{Faithful}: co-members that both pre-exist a step carry
the step template's net entry as their recorded value.  This is the
parent-pattern agreement the thirteenth review demanded; the round-23
catalogue generator will discharge it by construction.
\item \code{fresh\_tri}: \code{SCat} + \code{Faithful} + closure
below step $i$ yield the per-step triangle condition.  Four-way case
analysis on membership; the values are pinned by \code{member\_val}
and \code{steered\_val\_list} (uSource's clauses in list-row form,
bridged by \code{f\_reads\_rows}); the steered-steered case
instantiates the joint condition with the old pair's value
constrained through every separator member.
\item \code{closedBelow\_all} and \code{scat\_scond}: the induction
and the assembly --- the ROUND-22 MAIN THEOREM.  Corollary
\code{scat\_frame\_closed}: every frame triangle of a wellformed,
catalogue-checked, faithful certificate is composition-closed.
\item Witnesses: \code{certC\_scat}, \code{certC\_faithful}, an
\code{example} deriving \code{SCond certC} through the catalogue
route; \code{certD\_scat\_violated} --- the check fails at the
reachable row, matching \code{certD}'s actual frame violation.
\end{itemize}
