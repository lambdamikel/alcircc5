\begin{abstract}
This note reviews the round-8 ``saturated side context'' repair of GPT-5.5's
split-forest decidability proof for \ALCIRCC, as recorded in
\texttt{repaired\_split\_forest\_abstract\_round8\_repaired.tex} (15-page sketch)
and \texttt{expanded\_split\_forest\_full\_details\_round8\_full.tex} (30-page
companion) in \texttt{papers/claude\_latest\_review\_gpt5.5\_fix/}.  Round-8 was
written to close three defects of round-7 identified in a previous cold review
(folder \texttt{review2/}): missing side/tree pair exhaustion, the residual
$\DR/\PO$ frontier imposed before saturation, and pair/triple catalogues that
were bounded but not constructed.  We confirm that the round-7
blocking/equality repair (occurrence-sensitive incidence tags) is preserved and
correct, and that the soundness direction is essentially sound.  We then show
that the headline round-8 repair does \emph{not} close the side/tree gap: it
fixes the gap only on the \emph{finite, depth-bounded} vertical boundary
$B_k(u)$, whereas the genuinely hard case is an \emph{infinite} vertical tower
whose pumped tail the bounded saturated side context provably cannot reach.  We
exhibit a concrete satisfiable concept $C_0'$ for which RCC5 composition forces
a $\DR/\PO$ side witness to be a proper part of every node of an infinite
$\PP$-tower, and we verify (with a transcribed copy of the round-8 composition
table) that the constructed pair-shape catalogue contains \emph{no} shape that
can carry the forced label on the unbounded tail. The completeness construction
(Lemma~``split-cover'' and the extraction procedure) therefore produces an
\emph{invalid} certificate for $C_0'$, so completeness is not established as
written.  We argue the decidability \emph{theorem} probably survives via a
different presentation (placing the witness as a true vertical descendant of the
tower), but that presentation is neither constructed nor justified in the
manuscript.  We classify the defect as a real hole in the completeness proof of
medium--high severity, and give targeted repair suggestions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, method, and verdict}

\subsection{What was reviewed}

This review concerns the two round-8 manuscripts that the project
\texttt{README.md} and \texttt{CLAUDE.md} designate as the current canonical
no-automata statement of decidability (entry dated 2026-05-28, evening):
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{papers/claude\_latest\_review\_gpt5.5\_fix/}\\
      \texttt{repaired\_split\_forest\_abstract\_round8\_repaired.tex} --- the
      15-page sketch;
\item \texttt{papers/claude\_latest\_review\_gpt5.5\_fix/}\\
      \texttt{expanded\_split\_forest\_full\_details\_round8\_full.tex} --- the
      30-page detailed companion.
\end{itemize}
Both were read in full.  We also consulted the round-7 companion
(\texttt{expanded\_split\_forest\_full\_details\_proof.tex}, the subject of the
earlier \texttt{review2/}) for the round-7$\to$round-8 delta, and the project
\texttt{README.md} for the advertised status of the repair.  The round-8 README
news entry states that round-8 ``closes three defects identified by a cold
Claude review of round-7''; those three defects are precisely the major and
minor concerns of \texttt{review2/}.  The natural question for this review is
therefore: \emph{does the saturated-side-context construction actually close the
side/tree gap, or does it relocate it?}

As an explicit constraint from the prompt, nothing in
\texttt{alcircc5/} was modified.  All artifacts of this review live in
\texttt{review3/}: this document, a composition-checking script
\texttt{rcc5\_compose.py}, and its captured output \texttt{compose\_output.txt}.

\subsection{Method}

The composition arithmetic that underpins the central concern was transcribed
verbatim from the round-8 companion (its Section~2.1 table) into
\texttt{rcc5\_compose.py} and checked for inverse symmetry (zero violations) and
for the specific entries used below.  Every label propagation asserted in
Section~\ref{sec:central} of this review is reproduced and machine-checked by
that script.

\subsection{Verdict}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Soundness} (valid certificate $\Rightarrow$ model) is essentially
correct.  The occurrence-sensitive pair-shape machinery, the typed-equality
quotient, and the request-closed cycle argument correctly avoid the round-6
diagonal-$\EQ$ collapse.  We found no soundness counterexample.
\item \textbf{Completeness} (model $\Rightarrow$ valid certificate) is
\emph{not} established by the manuscript's construction.  The round-8 repair
saturates a \emph{finite, depth-bounded} side context and verticalizes only the
pairs that fall inside it.  We give a concrete satisfiable concept whose
honest model forces a $\DR/\PO$ side witness to be $\PP$-below an \emph{infinite}
pumped tower; the bounded side context cannot record the forced $\PP/\PPI$
labels on the tower's tail, and the constructed catalogue contains no admissible
pair shape for those pairs.  This is a genuine gap in the completeness proof
(Concern~D-1), not a cosmetic one.
\item \textbf{Theorem.}  We believe the decidability theorem is most likely
still \emph{true}: a different split presentation (witness as a true vertical
descendant of the tower, source--witness edge demoted to the residual $\PO$
frontier) appears to yield a valid certificate, and the decision procedure
accepts whenever \emph{any} valid certificate exists.  But the manuscript
neither constructs nor analyses that presentation; its stated construction is
the one that fails.  So the gap is in the \emph{proof}, with the theorem's
status reverting to ``strongly supported, not proven.''
\end{itemize}
We classify Concern~D-1 as \textbf{medium--high severity}: it directly breaks the
central completeness argument for an identifiable and natural class of concepts,
and it is exactly the obligation round-8 advertised as repaired.

\section{What round-8 gets right}

For balance, we first record the parts of the argument that we believe are
correct, several of them genuine improvements over round-7.

\paragraph{The blocking/equality repair is preserved and correct.}
The core round-7 move---labelling pairs by occurrence-sensitive shapes
$\alpha(u,v)=(s_u,p_u,s_v,p_v,\iota(u,v))$ with an incidence tag $\iota$ that
separates literal occurrence identity ($\self$) from same-profile repetition
across laps---is carried over intact (Def.~``incidence-tags'',
Lemma~``no-collapse-laps'', Lemma~``no accidental cycle equality'').  This
correctly prevents the round-6 collapse $L(\pi,\pi)=\EQ$ that conflated distinct
laps of a blocking cycle.  The worked stress case
$\exists\PP.\top\sqcap\forall\PP.\exists\PP.\top$ is handled correctly: laps
$u_i,u_j$ get tag $\up$ (label $\PP$), never $\self$.

\paragraph{Soundness is sound.}
Given a valid certificate, the unfolding/quotient construction
(Def.~``model-from-cert'', Lemmas ``well-defined-labels'',
``strong-eq-sound'', ``frame-sound'', ``truth'') builds a strong abstract RCC5
frame and proves a truth lemma.  Strong equality in the quotient is enforced
correctly because $\EQ$ arises only from $\self/\eqtag$ tags.  Composition in the
quotient is delegated to the triple-shape check (V8); since validity is checked
on the supplied finite catalogues, the soundness direction is internally
consistent.

\paragraph{The composition table is correct.}
We transcribed the round-8 table (companion Section~2.1) and verified it is
closed under the converse law $\comp(R,S)^{-1}=\comp(S^{-1},R^{-1})$ with zero
violations.  In particular the entries load-bearing below are confirmed:
\[
  \comp(\PPI,\PO)=\{\PO,\PPI\},\quad
  \comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\},\quad
  \comp(\PP,\PP)=\{\PP\}.
\]

\paragraph{Two of the three advertised round-7 repairs are real.}
The pair and triple catalogues are now \emph{constructed} as least sets under
explicit generation clauses (Defs.~``pair-shape'', ``triple-shape''), rather
than merely asserted finite; this genuinely addresses the round-7
``catalogues bounded but not constructed'' complaint (review2 MC-1/MC-4).  And
the residual $\DR/\PO$ restriction is now textually placed \emph{after} the
equality/containment closure step (Sat5)--(Sat6), addressing the
``restriction-before-saturation'' complaint (review2 MC-2(b)) at the level of
the local boundary.  The problem, developed next, is that these repairs operate
on a finite object that is too small to witness the obligation.

\section{Central concern (D-1): saturation is bounded; the obstruction is not}
\label{sec:central}

\subsection{The claim that is over-stated}

The round-8 abstract states (sketch, Lemma ``cross-side-tree-compact''; companion
Lemma~``cross-side-tree''):
\begin{quote}
``If $b$ is an exposed vertical boundary position of a source $u$ and $w$ is a
side position in $S_k(u)$, then the pair $(b,w)$ is either equality-linked,
placed in a local vertical stratum, or labelled $\DR$ or $\PO$\ldots In
particular, no $\PP/\PPI$ side/tree pair is hidden in the residual frontier.''
\end{quote}
Read literally, this lemma quantifies only over $b\in B_k(u)$, the
\emph{finite} vertical boundary of depth budget $k$, and over $w\in S_k(u)$, the
\emph{finite} saturated side context.  Both sets are bounded by
$m_*(C_0)$ (Lemma~``side-width'').  The lemma is true as stated.  The difficulty
is that the conclusion advertised in the README and the abstract --- ``no
$\PP/\PPI$ side/tree pair is hidden'' --- is a statement about the \emph{whole
unfolded model}, in which the vertical ancestor chain of $u$ is \emph{infinite}.
The lemma does not entail the advertised global statement, because the forced
$\PP/\PPI$ relationships extend up the infinite tower beyond $B_k(u)$.

\subsection{A concept that forces the unbounded case}

\begin{definition}[The stress concept]
Let $X$ and $Y$ be concept names.  Put
\[
  G \;:=\; (\forall\PO.X)\;\sqcap\;(\exists\PP.G),
\]
\[
  C_0' \;:=\; (\exists\PP.G)\;\sqcap\;(\exists\PO.\neg X).
\]
\end{definition}
The conjunct $\exists\PP.G$ inside $G$ makes $G$ generate an infinite ascending
chain of proper superparts $a_1\PP a_2\PP a_3\PP\cdots$, each $a_m\in G$, hence
each $a_m\in\forall\PO.X$.  The root $u$ (where $C_0'$ is evaluated) has a proper
superpart $a_1\in G$ and a $\PO$-witness $w\in\neg X$.  Note $u$ itself is
\emph{not} required to satisfy $\forall\PO.X$, so $u$ having a $\PO$-witness in
$\neg X$ is consistent.

\begin{observation}[$C_0'$ is satisfiable]
A concrete model: regions nested $a_1\subset a_2\subset a_3\subset\cdots$
(proper part $=$ subset), with $u\subset a_1$ and $w\subset a_1$ two
\emph{overlapping} subregions of $a_1$ (so $u\,\PO\,w$).  Then $u\PP a_m$ and
$w\PP a_m$ for all $m$.  Each $a_m$ has no $\PO$-neighbour among $\{u,w\}$ (both
are proper parts of $a_m$, not partial overlaps), so $\forall\PO.X$ at $a_m$ is
vacuously satisfied on these elements.  Hence $C_0'$ is satisfiable; in fact it
has no finite model (the ascending tower cannot terminate), which is exactly the
regime the split-forest blocking machinery exists to handle.
\end{observation}

\begin{lemma}[Composition forces $w$ to be a part of the entire tower]
\label{lem:forced}
In any model of $C_0'$ realising the generated witnesses, for every $m\ge1$ the
label $\rho(a_m,w)=\PPI$, i.e.\ $w$ is a proper part of every tower node $a_m$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Because $u\PP a_1$ we have $\rho(a_1,u)=\PPI$, and $\rho(u,w)=\PO$ by choice of
the $\PO$-witness.  Hence
\[
  \rho(a_1,w)\in\comp(\rho(a_1,u),\rho(u,w))=\comp(\PPI,\PO)=\{\PO,\PPI\}.
\]
Now $a_1\in\forall\PO.X$ while $w\in\neg X$; if $\rho(a_1,w)=\PO$ then $w\in X$,
a contradiction.  Therefore $\rho(a_1,w)=\PPI$.  For the inductive step,
$\rho(a_{m},a_{m-1})=\PPI$ (since $a_{m-1}\PP a_m$), so
\[
  \rho(a_m,w)\in\comp(\rho(a_m,a_{m-1}),\rho(a_{m-1},w))
            =\comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\}.
\]
Thus $\rho(a_m,w)=\PPI$ for all $m\ge1$.  The arithmetic
($\comp(\PPI,\PO)=\{\PO,\PPI\}$, $\comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\}$) is machine-checked
in \texttt{rcc5\_compose.py}.
\end{proof}

The essential point: the exclusion of $\PO$ in the base case uses a
\emph{universal} ($\forall\PO.X$) sitting on the tower, and the propagation up
the tower is \emph{forced} and \emph{unbounded}.  Crucially, $\DR$ is never
available: $\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PO)$ and $\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PPI)$.

\subsection{The constructed catalogue has no shape for the tail}

The completeness construction (companion Lemma~``split-cover'') attaches a
$\DR/\PO$ witness to its source as a \emph{saturated side context}: ``For a
selected $\DR$ or $\PO$ witness of a currently exposed occurrence, attach the
saturated context $S_k(u)$\ldots''  So $w$ is placed in $S_k(u)$, which by
Lemma~``side-width'' has at most $m_*(C_0')$ positions and reaches only finitely
far up the tower, say through $a_1,\dots,a_K$.  For $m>K$ the pair $(a_m,w)$ must
nevertheless carry the forced label $\PPI$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:forced}).  We check
every incidence tag the constructed pair catalogue (Def.~``pair-shape'') can
offer:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{$\sidetag_{\PPI}$} (Def.~``incidence-tags'': ``$u,v$ occur in one
saturated side context or local stratum''). Unavailable: $a_m\notin S_k(u)$ for
$m>K$, and $w$ lies in $S_k(u)$, attached low; the two never co-occur in one
bounded side context.

\item \textbf{$\down$} ($a_m$ is a strict equality-aware proper part \dots\ no:
$\down$ at $(a_m,w)$ means $w$ is a strict equality-aware proper part of $a_m$,
i.e.\ $a_m\reach_{\PPI}w$, i.e.\ $w\reach_{\PP}a_m$).  By
Def.~``eq-aware-vertical'' this requires an $\Eup$-path
$w\rightsquigarrow a_m$ in the \emph{selected cover relation}.  But $w$ was
selected as a $\PO$-witness of $u$; the split-cover construction adds no $\Eup$
cover edge out of $w$ into the tree.  Hence $w\reach_{\PP}a_m$ fails and the
$\down$ tag is unavailable.

\item \textbf{$\fronttag_{R}$} (residual frontier).  Restricted by construction
to $R\in\{\DR,\PO\}$ (mosaic axiom (M7), validity clause (V13),
Lemma~``residual-frontier'').  So a $\fronttag_{\PPI}$ shape does not exist.
\end{enumerate}

Thus the only catalogue shapes admissible for $(a_m,w)$, $m>K$, are
$\fronttag_{\DR}$ and $\fronttag_{\PO}$.  Both are impossible:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\fronttag_{\DR}$: the triple shape $(a_m,a_{m-1},w)$ (or
$(a_m,u,w)$ in the base case) must satisfy (V8); but
$\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PPI)$ (resp.\ $\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PO)$), so the triple is
RCC5-illegal and (V8) rejects it.
\item $\fronttag_{\PO}$: composition-legal in the base case
($\PO\in\comp(\PPI,\PO)$), but then $a_1$ sees $w$ as a $\PO$-successor, and
validity clause (V12) (``$\DR/\PO$ universal safety'') together with
$\forall\PO.X\in\tp(a_1)$ forces $X\in\tp(w)$.  Since $w\in\neg X$, (V12) fails.
For $m\ge2$ even composition-legality fails since $\PO\notin\comp(\PPI,\PPI)$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{proposition}[Completeness construction fails on $C_0'$]
\label{prop:fail}
For the satisfiable concept $C_0'$, the finite certificate produced by the
manuscript's stated completeness construction (Lemma~``split-cover'' followed by
the extraction procedure of Lemma~``extraction'') is invalid: at least one
ordered pair $(a_m,w)$ with $m$ larger than the side-width bound admits no
permitted pair shape with a label that simultaneously satisfies (V8) triple
composition and (V12) $\DR/\PO$ universal safety.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:forced} the forced label is $\PPI$.  By the case analysis
above, the constructed catalogue offers for $(a_m,w)$ only $\sidetag_{\PPI}$
(unavailable), $\down$ (unavailable), $\fronttag_{\DR}$ ((V8)-illegal), or
$\fronttag_{\PO}$ ((V12)-illegal, and for $m\ge2$ also (V8)-illegal).  Hence no
admissible shape carries the forced label, so the pair-shape-totality clause
(V6) cannot be met by the extracted object.  All shape verdicts are reproduced
in \texttt{rcc5\_compose.py}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Why this is not closed by the round-8 repair}

The round-8 repair is precisely the saturation of $S_k(u)$ and the
verticalization of side/tree pairs \emph{inside} it.  Proposition~\ref{prop:fail}
shows the obstruction lives \emph{outside} any bounded $S_k(u)$: it is the
interaction between a fixed side witness and the \emph{infinite pumped tail} of a
blocking tower.  Two structural facts make this unavoidable for the stated
construction:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the side-width bound (Lemma~``side-width'') makes $S_k(u)$ finite, so it
contains only finitely many tower nodes;
\item the forced $\PP/\PPI$ relationship to the tower is \emph{unbounded}
(Lemma~\ref{lem:forced}), and the only catalogue device that scales to an
unbounded vertical relation --- the $\up/\down$ tag via $\reach_{\PP}$ on $\Eup$
--- is not made available to a $\DR/\PO$ side witness, because the construction
attaches $w$ to $u$'s side context rather than splicing an $\Eup$ cover edge from
$w$ into the tree.
\end{enumerate}
So the gap is not ``a finite case we forgot to enumerate'' (the round-7
complaint, now genuinely fixed for finite boundaries); it is ``the finite
saturated object provably cannot witness an inherently infinite obligation.''

\section{The hidden ambiguity: do local strata join \texorpdfstring{$\Eup$}{E-up}?}
\label{sec:ambiguity}

The manuscript's escape route, if any, is the phrase ``add a local vertical
stratum containing the two endpoints'' in (Sat6).  Everything turns on whether a
local stratum edge is (a) a bounded mosaic-internal annotation tagged
$\sidetag_{\PP}$, or (b) a genuine addition to the global cover relation $\Eup$
that participates in $\reach_{\PP}$.

The textual evidence points to (a):
\begin{itemize}
\item $\reach_{\PP}$ is defined purely via $\Eup$, the ``selected upward
containment-cover relation'' (Def.~``weak-presentation'',
Def.~``eq-aware-vertical''); local strata are a separate component $\mathcal V$
of a mosaic (Def.~``mosaic'').
\item The incidence-tag table assigns pairs in a ``local stratum'' the tag
$\sidetag_R$, \emph{not} $\up/\down$ (Def.~``incidence-tags'').
\item Local strata are bounded objects inside the finite mosaic and are counted
by the side-width recurrence (Lemma~``side-width'').
\end{itemize}
Under reading (a), the verticalization stops at the mosaic boundary and
Proposition~\ref{prop:fail} stands.

If instead the intended meaning is (b) --- that verticalizing $(a_1,w)$ splices
an $\Eup$ edge $w\to a_1$ so that $\reach_{\PP}$ then carries $w$ up the entire
pumped tower, giving every $(a_m,w)$ the $\down$ tag --- then
Proposition~\ref{prop:fail} is avoided, but a different obligation appears and
is not discharged:
\begin{itemize}
\item Splicing $w$ below $a_1$ makes $w$ a true vertical descendant of the
tower, i.e.\ a node of the containment backbone, \emph{not} a side-mosaic
witness.  The construction would then have to demote the $u$--$w$ relation to a
\emph{residual $\PO$ frontier} between two proper parts of $a_1$, and re-derive
all of $w$'s own witnesses in the tree rather than in $u$'s side context.  The
manuscript does neither; its construction explicitly files $w$ under $u$'s side
context.
\item Under reading (b) the side-width bound argument is unsound as written,
because a single splice changes $w$'s vertical reach across an unbounded number
of laps, and the ``finite saturated neighbourhood'' framing no longer bounds the
relevant pairs (they are now handled by $\reach_{\PP}$, not by the mosaic).
\end{itemize}
Either way, the manuscript is \emph{underspecified at the load-bearing point}.
The single most useful clarification the authors could make is to state, with a
proof, exactly which relation receives the verticalizing edge and how
$\reach_{\PP}$ interacts with side-mosaic strata across blocking laps.

\section{Does the theorem survive? A plausible repair}
\label{sec:survive}

We believe $C_0'$ \emph{does} have a valid certificate, just not the one the
construction builds.  Place $w$ as a genuine vertical child of the tower node
$a_1$ (so $w\PP a_1$ via a selected $\Eup$ edge), make $u$ and $w$ two proper
parts of $a_1$ related by a \emph{residual $\PO$ frontier} edge, and let
$\reach_{\PP}$ carry $w$ up the pumped tower exactly as it carries $u$.  Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item every $(a_m,w)$ is a $\down$ pair with label $\PPI$ --- correct and
composition-legal;
\item the $u$--$w$ pair is $\fronttag_{\PO}$ --- legal, and (V12) for $u$ is
satisfied because $u$ carries no $\forall\PO$ obligation conflicting with $w$;
\item $a_m$'s $\forall\PO.X$ has no $\PO$-successor among $\{u,w\}$ (both are
$\PPI$ to $a_m$), so (V12) for the tower is vacuous on these positions.
\end{itemize}
Because the decision procedure accepts iff \emph{some} enumerated certificate is
valid, the existence of this presentation means the procedure most likely still
returns the correct verdict on $C_0'$.  Hence we expect Theorem~``main'' to be
\emph{true}.  But this argument is ours, not the manuscript's: the completeness
proof must \emph{construct} a valid certificate from a model, and the construction
it gives (saturated side context for every $\DR/\PO$ witness) is the one that
fails.  A correct completeness proof must replace ``attach every $\DR/\PO$
witness as a bounded side context'' by a rule that recognises when a $\DR/\PO$
witness is composition-forced into the vertical containment order of an ancestor
and routes it into the backbone instead.  This is a non-trivial change because
the decision of where to route $w$ depends on \emph{universals on unboundedly
many ancestors}, which is global information not visible in the local side
context.

\section{Secondary observations}

\paragraph{(S-1) The cross side/tree exhaustion lemma is correct but mis-scoped.}
As noted, Lemma~``cross-side-tree'' is true for $b\in B_k(u)$ but is used (in the
README and abstract) to justify a global ``no hidden $\PP/\PPI$'' claim it does
not support.  The remedy is to state the lemma's quantifier honestly and add the
separate, harder argument for the infinite tail (Section~\ref{sec:survive}).

\paragraph{(S-2) The ``constructed catalogue'' repair is orthogonal to the real
gap.}
Round-8 correctly upgrades $\Pair_\Q$ and $\Trip_\Q$ from ``finite'' to
``constructed least sets.''  But Proposition~\ref{prop:fail} shows the
constructed set is constructed to be \emph{incomplete}: it has no shape for the
forced deep pairs.  Making a catalogue explicit does not make it exhaustive over
the unfolded model; the exhaustion claim (Lemma~``pair-triple-exhaustion'') silently
assumes every $\PP/\PPI$ pair is either $\reach_{\PP}$-related or co-resident in a
bounded side context, which is exactly what fails here.

\paragraph{(S-3) The replacement lemma needs an identity-preservation clause.}
Lemma~``replacement'' records, for a component $K$, ``the set of strict $\PP$ and
$\PPI$ target \emph{types} reachable inside $K$.''  A fixed side witness that is a
common proper part of an unbounded pumped chain is an interaction in which the
\emph{identity} of the shared part (one $w$ for all laps), not merely the
existence of a part of that type, is constrained by tower universals
($\forall\PPI.\,\cdot$ in a variant of $C_0'$).  Pumping with ``fresh copies''
preserves type-reachability but can silently replace ``one shared $w$'' by ``one
$w$ per lap.''  For $C_0'$ this happens to be harmless, but the lemma is stated
in full generality and the identity constraint is not addressed.

\paragraph{(S-4) Truth-lemma converse for $\exists\PP$ is phrased loosely.}
In Lemma~``truth'', the converse direction for $\exists\PP.E$ concludes
$\exists\PP.E\in\tp(u)$ from the existence of a $\PP$-successor satisfying $E$ via
``type legality plus witness support.''  The honest justification is the
contrapositive of vertical universal safety (V10): if $\exists\PP.E\notin\tp(u)$
then $\forall\PP.\overline E\in\tp(u)$, and (V10) forbids a $\PP$-successor
satisfying $E$.  The conclusion is recoverable but the stated reasoning is
circular as written.  Cosmetic.

\paragraph{(S-5) No complexity claim --- appropriate.}
Consistent with \texttt{CLAUDE.md}, the manuscript commits only to an effectively
computable certificate bound and asserts no elementary upper bound for the full
logic.  We agree this is the right posture and flag no over-claim here.

\section{Recommendations}

\begin{enumerate}[label=R\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Re-route composition-forced witnesses into the backbone.}  Replace
the rule ``attach every $\DR/\PO$ witness as a bounded saturated side context''
by a rule that first tests whether RCC5 composition with the source's ancestor
universals forces the witness to be $\PP/\PPI$-comparable with some ancestor; if
so, place the witness as a vertical relative (with a selected $\Eup$ edge) and
demote the source--witness edge to a residual $\DR/\PO$ frontier.  This is the
presentation of Section~\ref{sec:survive}.

\item \textbf{State the verticalization/$\Eup$ interaction precisely.}  Resolve
the ambiguity of Section~\ref{sec:ambiguity}: declare whether local strata
contribute to $\reach_{\PP}$, and if so re-do the side-width bound accordingly
(it must now bound \emph{cover edges added}, not \emph{pairs co-resident in a
mosaic}).

\item \textbf{Prove a global ``forced verticalization'' lemma.}  Show: for every
$\DR/\PO$ witness $w$ of $u$ and every ancestor $a$ of $u$, the label
$\rho(a,w)$ is determined by $\rho(a,u)$, $\rho(u,w)$, and the $\forall\PO/\forall\DR$
content of $\tp(a)$; tabulate the finitely many outcomes; and route $w$ into the
backbone in exactly the $\PP/\PPI$ outcomes.  This converts the unbounded
obstruction into a finite case analysis on \emph{one} ancestor relation, after
which $\reach_{\PP}$ handles the tail.

\item \textbf{Add $C_0'$ (and its UNSAT sibling
$G:=\forall\PO.X\sqcap\forall\PPI.Y\sqcap\exists\PP.G$,
$C_0:=\exists\PP.G\sqcap\exists\PO.(\neg X\sqcap\neg Y)$) to the verification
suite}, as constructive certificate-emission tests analogous to the existing
\texttt{wp7/wp8/wp9} scripts, so that the regression is caught mechanically.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Round-8 correctly preserves the round-7 blocking/equality repair, has a sound
soundness direction, and genuinely upgrades two of the three round-7
deficiencies (constructed catalogues; residual restriction textually after
saturation).  However, the headline repair --- saturated side contexts with
cross side/tree verticalization --- closes the side/tree gap only on the finite
vertical boundary.  The mathematically essential case is a $\DR/\PO$ witness that
RCC5 composition forces to be a proper part of an \emph{infinite} pumped tower
(Lemma~\ref{lem:forced}), and the bounded saturated side context provably cannot
record the forced $\PP/\PPI$ labels on the tower's tail
(Proposition~\ref{prop:fail}).  The completeness construction as written
therefore does not produce a valid certificate for the satisfiable concept
$C_0'$.  We expect the decidability \emph{theorem} to survive via the alternative
presentation of Section~\ref{sec:survive}, but that presentation is not the one
the manuscript constructs, so the completeness \emph{proof} has a genuine hole of
medium--high severity.  The recommendations above indicate a concrete path to a
repair that we believe would close it.
