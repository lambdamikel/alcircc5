Composition-Forced Verticalization}
\author{Independent repair note (Claude, Anthropic), prompted by Michael Wessel}
\date{28 May 2026}

\begin{abstract}
The companion review (\texttt{review3/review\_round8\_split\_forest.pdf})
identified a gap (D-1) in the completeness direction of GPT-5.5's round-8
saturated-side-context proof: a $\DR/\PO$ side witness can be RCC5-composition
forced to be a proper part of an \emph{infinite} pumped containment tower, and
the bounded saturated side context cannot record the forced $\PP/\PPI$ labels on
the tower's tail, so the certificate produced by the stated construction is
invalid.  This note supplies a repair.  The repair rests on one principle ---
the vertical backbone must present the \emph{full} generated containment order,
not merely the selected-witness edges --- realised by a single new operation
(splicing a composition-forced witness into the cover relation $\Eup$ at a
bounded height).  We prove the three facts the repair needs: (A) the forced
label up an ascending chain is monotone and eventually constant
(\emph{threshold} lemma, machine-checked); (B) the threshold is bounded by the
number of boundary descriptors, so finiteness of the certificate universe is
preserved; and (C/D) the repaired presentation embeds label-preservingly into
the fixed satisfying model, so RCC5 composition and universal safety are
inherited rather than re-derived, and the descriptor space stays finite.  We
restate the patched split-cover lemma and the restored completeness theorem,
re-derive the stress concept $C_0'$ (now certifiable) and its UNSAT sibling
(still correctly rejected), and close with an honest list of the obligations a
referee should still check.  All composition arithmetic and the two worked
certificates are machine-checked by the accompanying scripts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Purpose and summary}

We work entirely inside the framework of the round-8 expanded companion
\texttt{expanded\_split\_forest\_full\_details\_round8\_full.tex}; definitions and
validity clauses are cited by their names there
(``thinning'', ``split-cover'', ``eq-aware-vertical'', ``incidence-tags'',
``pair-shape'', ``side-width'', ``replacement'', ``blocking'', and the validity
conditions (V1)--(V15)).  Nothing in \texttt{alcircc5/} is modified; this is a
standalone replacement for the witness-placement step of the completeness proof.

The gap, in one sentence: the round-8 construction places every $\DR/\PO$ witness
$w$ of a source $u$ inside a \emph{bounded} saturated side context $S_k(u)$, but
RCC5 composition can force $w$ to be $\PP/\PPI$-comparable with \emph{unboundedly
many} vertical ancestors of $u$, and a bounded side context cannot carry those
forced labels (companion review, Proposition~``fail'').

The repair, in one sentence: do not file such a $w$ under $u$'s side context;
instead detect the composition-forced comparability and \emph{splice} $w$ into the
vertical cover relation $\Eup$ at the (provably bounded) height where it first
becomes a proper part, so that the equality-aware reachability $\reach_\PP$ ---
which already scales to infinite towers --- carries the correct $\PP/\PPI$ label
to the entire tail.

The conceptual reason the repair is clean: the completeness construction is
\emph{presenting a fixed, already-consistent model} $\J$, not synthesising a new
one.  The bug was presentational (the forest had no slot for a
composition-forced vertical pair), not a failure of consistency.  A presentation
that routes the pair correctly inherits RCC5 composition and universal safety
\emph{from $\J$} for free.

\section{The generated order and the presentation principle}

Fix a witness-generated model $\J=(\W,\rho)$ obtained from the thinning lemma,
with $\W$ countable and every closure existential witnessed inside $\W$.  Write
$x<y$ for $\rho(x,y)=\PP$ (``$x$ is a proper part of $y$'').

\begin{proposition}[The generated containment order]\label{prop:order}
$(\W,<)$ is a strict partial order: irreflexive, transitive, and antisymmetric.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Irreflexivity: $\rho(x,x)=\EQ\ne\PP$ by strong equality.  Transitivity: if
$x<y<z$ then $\rho(x,z)\in\comp(\PP,\PP)=\{\PP\}$, so $x<z$.  Antisymmetry: if
$x<y$ and $y<x$ then $\rho(x,y)=\PP$ and $\rho(y,x)=\PP$; but
$\rho(y,x)=\Inv(\rho(x,y))=\PPI\ne\PP$, a contradiction.  (All three use only
the frame axioms and $\comp(\PP,\PP)=\{\PP\}$.)
\end{proof}

\begin{principle}[Faithful backbone]\label{prin:backbone}
The vertical backbone of the split presentation must generate, under
equality-aware transitive closure, \emph{the entire restriction of $<$ to the
generated occurrences}, not merely the sub-relation given by selected
$\PP/\PPI$ witness edges.
\end{principle}

The round-8 construction violates Principle~\ref{prin:backbone}: it builds
$\Eup$ only from selected $\PP/\PPI$ witnesses, so a generated element $w$ that
happens (by composition) to satisfy $w<a$ for some ancestor $a$ --- without $a$
having been \emph{selected} as a $\PP$-witness of $w$ --- has no $\Eup$-path to
$a$, and the pair $(a,w)$ is left to the residual frontier, where the label
$\PP/\PPI$ is illegal.  The repair restores Principle~\ref{prin:backbone} by
adding exactly the missing cover edges, at bounded height.

\section{Corrected witness placement}

\subsection{Where composition forces verticality}

\begin{definition}[Ancestor profile of a side pair]\label{def:anc-profile}
Let $u\in\W$ have a selected $\DR/\PO$ witness $w$ (so $\rho(u,w)\in\{\DR,\PO\}$),
and let $a$ be a $<$-ancestor of $u$ (so $u<a$, i.e.\ $\rho(a,u)=\PPI$).  The
\emph{ancestor relation} of the pair at $a$ is $r_a:=\rho(a,w)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Forced range]\label{lem:forced-range}
In the situation of Definition~\ref{def:anc-profile}, $r_a\in\{\DR,\PO,\PPI\}$;
in particular $r_a\notin\{\EQ,\PP\}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$r_a\ne\EQ$: else $a=w$, but $\rho(a,u)=\PPI$ while $\rho(w,u)=\Inv(\rho(u,w))\in
\{\DR,\PO\}$.  $r_a\ne\PP$: $\rho(a,w)=\PP$ means $a<w$; with $u<a$ this gives
$u<w$ by Proposition~\ref{prop:order}, so $\rho(u,w)=\PP$, contradicting
$\rho(u,w)\in\{\DR,\PO\}$.  The remaining base labels are $\DR,\PO,\PPI$.
\end{proof}

Thus a side pair is comparable with an ancestor exactly when $r_a=\PPI$ (then
$w<a$, $w$ a proper part of $a$).  We must route $w$ into the backbone precisely
on the set $\{a: r_a=\PPI\}$.

\subsection{The splice}

\begin{definition}[Corrected split-cover with splicing]\label{def:corrected}
Construct the weak split presentation as in companion Lemma~``split-cover'',
with one change.  When a selected $\DR/\PO$ witness $w$ of $u$ is generated, also
inspect the ancestor relations $r_a$ for the $<$-ancestors $a$ of $u$.  Let
\[
   A_\PPI(w)=\{a : u<a \text{ and } r_a=\PPI\}=\{a: w<a\}.
\]
If $A_\PPI(w)\ne\varnothing$, let $a^\ast$ be a $<$-minimal element of
$A_\PPI(w)$ (it exists and is unique on the ancestor chain of $u$, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:threshold} below).  Add the cover edge $w\,\Eup\,a^\ast$ (the
\emph{splice}), so that $w$ becomes a vertical proper part of $a^\ast$ and, by
transitivity of $\Eup$, of every $a\in A_\PPI(w)$.  Demote the pair $(u,w)$ and
every pair $(a,w)$ with $a\notin A_\PPI(w)$ to its inherited $\{\DR,\PO\}$
residual/side label.  If $A_\PPI(w)=\varnothing$, leave $w$ as a pure side
witness exactly as in round-8.
\end{definition}

The splice edge $w\,\Eup\,a^\ast$ asserts the genuine fact $w<a^\ast$ of $\J$, so
it is sound by construction; the next sections show it is well-defined,
bounded, and globally consistent.

\section{The threshold lemmas}

\subsection{Monotonicity}

\begin{lemma}[Monotone threshold]\label{lem:threshold}
Let $w$ be a $\DR/\PO$ witness of $u$, and let $a_1<a_2<a_3<\cdots$ be the
$<$-ancestor chain of $u$ (so $u<a_1$ and $a_m<a_{m+1}$).  Put $r_m=\rho(a_m,w)$.
Then, with respect to the linear order $\DR\succ\PO\succ\PPI$, the sequence
$(r_m)$ is non-increasing:
\[
   r_{m+1}\in\comp(\PPI,r_m)\quad\text{and}\quad r_{m+1}\preceq r_m .
\]
Consequently $(r_m)$ is eventually constant; there are thresholds
$1\le k_1\le k_2\le\infty$ with $r_m=\DR$ for $m<k_1$, $r_m=\PO$ for
$k_1\le m<k_2$, and $r_m=\PPI$ for $m\ge k_2$.  In particular $\{a:w<a\}$ is an
upward-closed final segment of the chain with $<$-minimum $a_{k_2}$ (when
$k_2<\infty$).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$\rho(a_{m+1},a_m)=\PPI$ since $a_m<a_{m+1}$, so
$r_{m+1}\in\comp(\rho(a_{m+1},a_m),\rho(a_m,w))=\comp(\PPI,r_m)$.  By
Lemma~\ref{lem:forced-range} each $r_m\in\{\DR,\PO,\PPI\}$, and the relevant
transitions are
\[
  \comp(\PPI,\DR)=\{\DR,\PO,\PPI\},\quad
  \comp(\PPI,\PO)=\{\PO,\PPI\},\quad
  \comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\}.
\]
Inspection shows no transition increases the rank in $\DR\succ\PO\succ\PPI$:
from $\DR$ one stays $\preceq\DR$; from $\PO$ the options $\{\PO,\PPI\}$ are
$\preceq\PO$ (note $\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PO)$, so no return to $\DR$); from
$\PPI$ the only option is $\PPI$.  A non-increasing sequence in a three-element
order is eventually constant, giving the thresholds.  Upward-closure of
$\{a:w<a\}$ is $\PPI$-absorption ($\comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\}$).
\end{proof}

All three transition entries, the non-increase, and the $\PPI$-absorption are
machine-checked in \texttt{fix\_feasibility.py}.

\begin{remark}[The benign regimes]
If $k_2=\infty$ the witness is never a proper part of an ancestor; then every
$(a_m,w)$ carries an inherited $\DR$ or $\PO$ label, which is a legal
residual/side shape, and (V12) holds because the labels are inherited from the
model $\J$.  There is \emph{no gap} in these regimes; the round-8 construction
already handles them.  The repair is needed only when $k_2<\infty$.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Boundedness}

For finiteness we must bound $k_2$.  Recall the boundary descriptor of companion
Definition~``boundary-descriptor'' and the replacement/blocking lemmas
(``replacement'', ``blocking''); a checkpoint of the chain is a generated
component, and there are at most $N$ distinct boundary descriptors for fixed
$C_0$, where $N\le 2^{\mathrm{poly}(n)}$ is the round-8 descriptor bound.

\begin{lemma}[Bounded threshold]\label{lem:bounded}
The chain may be re-presented, preserving all closure formulas and finite-prefix
RCC5 composition, so that the splice height satisfies $k_2\le 3N$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:threshold} the chain splits into three consecutive
constant-$r$ stretches: a $\DR$-stretch $[1,k_1)$, a $\PO$-stretch $[k_1,k_2)$,
and the $\PPI$-tail $[k_2,\infty)$.  Consider any one stretch.  Along it the
relation $r_m$ to the fixed occurrence $w$ is constant, so the value of $r_m$ is
not part of what distinguishes checkpoints within the stretch; the only
distinguishing data is the boundary descriptor, of which there are at most $N$.
If a stretch has more than $N$ checkpoints, two of them, say at heights $i<j$,
have equal boundary descriptors.  By the replacement lemma the segment
$[i,j)$ may be excised and $a_{i-1}$ glued directly to $a_j$, preserving all
closure formulas and finite-prefix composition.  Because $r$ is constant on the
stretch, $r_{i-1}=r_j$, so the glue is coherent with the relation to $w$ as
well: the inherited label between $w$ and the glued checkpoint is unchanged.
Iterating, each stretch is shortened to at most $N$ checkpoints, so
$k_1\le N$ and $k_2\le 2N<3N$.  (The bound $3N$ leaves room for the single
glued boundary checkpoints between stretches.)  The $\PPI$-tail is then a
standard blocking region: $r=\PPI$ is constant, and ordinary blocking applies.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Periodic witness families]\label{rem:periodic}
Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded} bounds the splice height for a \emph{single} fixed
witness $w$.  When a whole \emph{periodic family} of witnesses occurs --- e.g.\
every tower node carries its own $\DR/\PO$ witness that becomes its proper part
--- the splice is not placed in the prefix but is incorporated into the
recurrent cyclic profile itself (``this profile splices a proper-part side child
here''), and pumping reproduces one fresh spliced witness per lap.  Cross-lap
pairs between these fresh witnesses are ordinary occurrence-sensitive pairs.
Either way the certificate stays finite.
\end{remark}

\section{Consistency and finiteness are inherited from \texorpdfstring{$\J$}{J}}

\begin{lemma}[Acyclicity of the spliced backbone]\label{lem:acyclic}
The equality-aware transitive closure $\reach_\PP$ of the spliced cover relation
$\Eup$ is a strict partial order; in particular the splice introduces no
containment cycle.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Every $\Eup$ edge --- selected or spliced --- asserts a genuine relation $x<y$
of $\J$ (the splice edge $w\,\Eup\,a^\ast$ asserts $w<a^\ast$, which holds
because $a^\ast\in A_\PPI(w)$).  Equality mates have, by construction, identical
$<$-relations to all occurrences (typed congruence).  Hence
$\reach_\PP\subseteq{<}$ on origins, and ${<}$ is a strict partial order by
Proposition~\ref{prop:order}.  A sub-relation of a strict partial order is
acyclic.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Label-preserving embedding]\label{lem:embed}
The quotient of the corrected presentation by explicit equality maps
label-preservingly onto the generated submodel of $\J$.  Consequently every
RCC5-composition obligation and every universal-safety obligation of the
certificate holds, because it holds in $\J$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each occurrence has an origin in $\W$; equality mates share an origin; the
splice changes only \emph{which presentation device} carries a pair's label
(a vertical $\up/\down$ shape instead of an impossible residual shape), never
the label itself, which is inherited from $\rho$.  After the splice, every pair
$(a,w)$ with $w<a$ has $\reach_\PP(w,a)$ (Definition~\ref{def:corrected}), hence
the $\down$ shape with label $\PPI=\rho(a,w)$; every pair with $r_a\in\{\DR,\PO\}$
keeps its inherited residual/side label.  Thus the map origin$:\;$occurrence
$\mapsto\W$ is a label isomorphism onto the generated submodel.  Composition
(V8) for any triple of occurrences is the composition of their origins in $\J$;
universal safety (V10)/(V12) for any pair is the universal it satisfies in $\J$.
Both transfer.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Descriptor space stays finite]\label{lem:desc-finite}
The repair does not enlarge the descriptor universe beyond a finite bound.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A boundary descriptor records reachable \emph{types} (subsets of $\Cl(C_0)$,
$\le 2^n$ of them) and side-mosaic \emph{isomorphism types} (finitely many by
the side-width bound), not individual occurrences.  Splicing adds, to a
checkpoint's data, at most the finite list of incident splice-cover edges; each
such edge is described by the (bounded) profile/port of its endpoints and one of
five labels.  In the recurrent tail the relation to spliced witnesses is the
constant $\PPI$ and contributes only the presence of the witness type in the
downward type-set --- already a bounded datum.  Hence the descriptor space is
multiplied by a bounded factor and remains finite; the round-8 finiteness
counts (Lemmas ``side-width'', ``finite-universe'') are preserved up to that
factor.
\end{proof}

\section{Compatibility with split copies (overlap)}

A side witness $w$ may be forced to share a proper part with its source.

\begin{lemma}[Overlap reduces to split copies]\label{lem:overlap}
Let $w$ be a $\DR/\PO$ witness of $u$ and $d<u$ a generated descendant.  Then
$\rho(w,d)\in\comp(\rho(w,u),\rho(u,d))=\comp(\{\DR,\PO\},\PPI)$; in the $\PO$
case $\rho(w,d)\in\{\DR,\PO,\PPI\}$.  If $\rho(w,d)=\PPI$ (so $d<w$ as well as
$d<u$), then $d$ is a common proper part of the $\PO$-incomparable elements $u$
and $w$, and is represented by the existing split-copy discipline: occurrences
$d^{(1)}\,\Eup$-below $u$ and $d^{(2)}\,\Eup$-below $w$ with $d^{(1)}\eqc
d^{(2)}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The composition values are read off the round-8 table ($\comp(\PO,\PPI)=
\{\DR,\PO,\PPI\}$, $\comp(\DR,\PPI)=\{\DR\}$; machine-checked).  When
$\rho(w,d)=\PPI$, $d<u$ and $d<w$ hold simultaneously while $\rho(u,w)\in\{\DR,
\PO\}$, which is exactly the configuration the round-8 split-copy discipline
(companion Lemma ``multiple-superpart-split'') was designed for: a single origin
$d$ placed under two $<$-incomparable parents via equality-mate occurrences.
Typed congruence makes the two copies carry identical labels to all third
occurrences, and quotienting recovers the single origin $d$.  The set of such
common parts is a subset of the generated descendants of $u$, finite per
generated component, so no new infinity is introduced.
\end{proof}

\section{Patched statements}

\begin{definition}[Patched incidence of spliced pairs]\label{def:patched-tags}
In companion Definition ``incidence-tags'', a pair $(u,v)$ that is
$\reach_\PP$-related \emph{through a splice edge} receives the vertical tag
$\up$ (resp.\ $\down$) and hence the mandatory label $\PP$ (resp.\ $\PPI$),
exactly as for selected vertical pairs.  The tag $\sidetag_R$ is reserved for
pairs inside one bounded saturated context; it is \emph{not} used for a
$\PP/\PPI$ pair that the splice has placed in $\reach_\PP$.  This removes the
round-8 ambiguity (review3 \S4) by declaring that verticalizing a forced pair
contributes to the global $\reach_\PP$, not merely to a local mosaic.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Patched split-cover]\label{lem:patched-splitcover}
Definition~\ref{def:corrected} produces a weak split presentation whose strong
quotient is label-isomorphic to the generated submodel of $\J$, in which
\emph{every} pair with origin label $\PP/\PPI$ is realised by equality-aware
vertical reachability (selected or spliced), every $\EQ$ pair by explicit
equality, and every residual/side pair by an inherited $\DR/\PO$ label.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Round-8 Lemma ``split-cover'' already realises selected $\PP/\PPI$ witnesses,
$\EQ$ mates, and $\DR/\PO$ side witnesses.  The only pairs it failed to realise
were $(a,w)$ with $w<a$ for a $\DR/\PO$ witness $w$ and a non-selected ancestor
$a$ (the D-1 pairs).  By Definition~\ref{def:corrected} the splice makes
$\reach_\PP(w,a)$ hold for every such $a$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:threshold}:
$\{a:w<a\}$ is the final segment above $a^\ast$, and $a^\ast\,\Eup$-reaches all
of it), so these pairs now carry the $\down$ shape with the correct label
$\PPI$.  Acyclicity is Lemma~\ref{lem:acyclic}; label-preservation is
Lemma~\ref{lem:embed}; overlap is Lemma~\ref{lem:overlap}.  Hence every origin
$\PP/\PPI$ pair is vertical, as claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Validity clauses]
No validity clause is weakened.  The only structural change visible to the
checker is that some pairs previously destined for the residual frontier are now
$\up/\down$ pairs; (V6) pair-shape determinacy, (V8) composition, and (V10)
vertical universal safety apply to them unchanged.  Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded}
keeps every bound in (V15) finite.  Optionally one adds a single bookkeeping
clause:

\smallskip
\noindent(V16) \emph{Forced verticalization.}  For every $\DR/\PO$ witness $w$
of a source $u$ and every ancestor $a$ of $u$ with $\rho(a,w)=\PPI$ in the
recorded labels, $w\,\reach_\PP\,a$ holds.

\smallskip
\noindent This clause is decidable (a finite reachability test) and is exactly
what the splice guarantees.  It turns the D-1 obstruction into a checkable
condition.
\end{remark}

\section{Completeness restored}

\begin{theorem}[Completeness, repaired]\label{thm:complete}
If $C_0$ is satisfiable, then $C_0$ has a valid finite generated split-forest
certificate (in the sense of the round-8 companion, augmented by
Definition~\ref{def:patched-tags} and optionally (V16)).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $\J,a_0\models C_0$ be witness-generated (thinning lemma).  Build the
corrected split presentation (Definition~\ref{def:corrected}); it is a faithful
label-preserving presentation of the generated submodel
(Lemma~\ref{lem:patched-splitcover}).  Extract a finite certificate by the
round-8 extraction procedure (companion Lemma ``extraction''), with two changes:
(i) splice edges are recorded as cover edges of $\Eup$, contributing to the
$\up/\down$ pair shapes; (ii) per-witness splice heights are normalised to
$\le 3N$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded}, or absorbed into recurrent profiles by
Remark~\ref{rem:periodic}.  All validity clauses hold: type legality and witness
support from satisfaction in $\J$; equality congruence from equality of origins;
RCC5 composition and universal safety by Lemma~\ref{lem:embed}; request-closure
and universal safety on cycles from the blocking lemma applied to the $\PPI$-tail
and the bounded prefix; finiteness from Lemmas~\ref{lem:bounded} and
\ref{lem:desc-finite}.  The extracted object is therefore a valid certificate.
\end{proof}

Together with round-8 soundness (untouched by this note) and the finite,
effective enumeration (preserved by Lemma~\ref{lem:desc-finite}), this restores
the decision procedure and hence Theorem~``main'' of the round-8 companion.

\section{Worked cases}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$C_0'$}{C0'}: a valid certificate now exists}

Recall $G=(\forall\PO.X)\sqcap(\exists\PP.G)$ and
$C_0'=(\exists\PP.G)\sqcap(\exists\PO.\neg X)$ from review3.  The honest model
has $u$ with a $\PO$-witness $w\in\neg X$ and an ascending tower
$a_1<a_2<\cdots$, each $a_m\in\forall\PO.X$, with $\rho(a_m,w)=\PPI$ for all $m$
(machine-checked: $\comp(\PPI,\PO)=\{\PO,\PPI\}$, $\forall\PO.X$ kills $\PO$,
$\comp(\PPI,\PPI)=\{\PPI\}$ propagates).  Here $k_2=1$, so the splice is
$w\,\Eup\,a_1$.  The accompanying \texttt{repaired\_certificate.py} builds this
presentation explicitly on a depth-$8$ prefix and verifies:
\begin{itemize}
\item zero RCC5 frame-axiom violations on every triple;
\item $\reach_\PP(w,a_m)$ holds for every tower node, so the $\down$ shape with
label $\PPI$ is available for every $(a_m,w)$;
\item $(u,w)$ is residual with the inherited label $\PO$;
\item every demand of $C_0'$ is met, the single top existential being discharged
by the blocking cycle.
\end{itemize}
Thus the repaired construction yields a valid certificate, exactly the object the
round-8 construction failed to produce.

\subsection{The UNSAT sibling: still correctly rejected}

Let $G=(\forall\PO.X)\sqcap(\forall\PPI.Y)\sqcap(\exists\PP.G)$ and
$C_0=(\exists\PP.G)\sqcap(\exists\PO.(\neg X\sqcap\neg Y))$.  Now $w\in\neg X\cap
\neg Y$.  The splice makes $w$ a $\PPI$-successor (proper part) of $a_1$, so
(V10) fires $a_1$'s $\forall\PPI.Y$ on $w$, requiring $Y\in\tp(w)$ ---
contradicting $w\in\neg Y$.  The alternative residual label $\PO$ fires
$\forall\PO.X$ via (V12), requiring $X\in\tp(w)$, contradicting $w\in\neg X$;
and $\DR$ is composition-illegal ($\DR\notin\comp(\PPI,\PO)$).
\texttt{repaired\_certificate.py} enumerates the two composition-legal labels for
$(a_1,w)$ and confirms both clash, so no valid certificate exists and the
procedure correctly returns UNSAT.  The repair therefore does not over-accept:
it adds the missing $\PP/\PPI$ pair shapes \emph{and} the universal-safety
obligations that come with them.

\section{Scope and remaining verification obligations}

The repair is local to the witness-placement step; soundness, the
occurrence-sensitive blocking machinery, the typed-equality quotient, and the
request-closed cycle argument are untouched.  We have proved monotonicity
(Lemma~\ref{lem:threshold}), boundedness (Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded}), acyclicity
(Lemma~\ref{lem:acyclic}), inherited consistency (Lemma~\ref{lem:embed}), and
finiteness (Lemma~\ref{lem:desc-finite}); the algebra and two worked
certificates are machine-checked.  A careful referee should still verify:

\begin{enumerate}[label=O\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{Excision coherence under replacement.}  Lemma~\ref{lem:bounded}
excises a same-descriptor segment of the chain while a fixed off-chain witness
$w$ hangs below it.  We argued the inherited label $r$ to $w$ is constant on the
stretch, hence preserved; one should confirm the replacement lemma's
preconditions (boundary descriptor identity) genuinely subsume the labels to
spliced-in witnesses crossing the boundary, i.e.\ that the descriptor as defined
records enough to license the excision in the presence of splices.  This is the
single most load-bearing point.

\item \textbf{Simultaneous splices.}  Lemmas~\ref{lem:embed} and
\ref{lem:desc-finite} treat the splices collectively via the embedding into
$\J$, but the interaction of many splices with the blocking decomposition (which
splice lands in the prefix vs.\ in a recurrent profile, per
Remark~\ref{rem:periodic}) deserves a fully spelled-out normal form.

\item \textbf{Overlap amalgam closure.}  Lemma~\ref{lem:overlap} reduces the
shared-part case to existing split copies; one should check that the
support-closed mosaic family (companion Definition ``support-closed'') remains
closed when a side witness is simultaneously a backbone node and a $\PO$-relative
of its former source.

\item \textbf{No interaction with the round-8 finiteness constants.}  The
descriptor blow-up factor in Lemma~\ref{lem:desc-finite} should be carried
through the explicit bounds of the round-8 enumeration appendix to confirm the
universe is still effectively enumerable (it is elementary, but the constant
changes).
\end{enumerate}

None of these is an obstruction in the sense of D-1: they are bookkeeping
obligations on a construction that, by Lemma~\ref{lem:embed}, presents a fixed
consistent model and therefore cannot violate composition or universal safety.
We accordingly assess the decidability theorem as \emph{true and provable along
this route}, with the present note supplying the missing completeness argument
modulo the bookkeeping items O1--O4.
