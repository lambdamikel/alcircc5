\section*{Overall assessment}

I agree with the referee's central criticism.  The previous automata proof had the right
high-level architecture, but the finite abstraction layer was not adequately justified.
In particular, the proof attempted to recover a global RCC5 labelling from local
pair/triple transformer states.  The missing step was a confluence theorem saying that
initialising at an ancestor and pushing endpoints down gives the same pair relation as
initialising at the least common interface.  That theorem had not been proved, and the
local transition checks did not enforce it.

The corrected proof therefore removes the transformer construction.  The replacement is
a patchwork-bag abstraction: each finite bag carries a complete atomic RCC5 network,
adjacent bags agree on overlaps, and global RCC5 consistency follows from the patchwork
property of RCC5.  Equality is no longer handled by quotienting repeated blocking copies;
it is handled as exact occurrence identity in a tree decomposition.  The automaton is
also made explicitly two-way, and universal propagation is checked with exact
relation-vector shadows rather than a may-analysis.

The repaired proof is supplied separately as
\texttt{split\_forest\_patchwork\_automata\_repaired.tex/pdf}.

\section*{What remains from the original split-forest abstraction}

The repaired proof does not abandon the split-forest idea.  It keeps the split forest as
the semantic normal form:
\[
\text{witness thinning}\quad\to\quad\text{generated containment covers}\quad\to\quad
\text{saturated side contexts}.
\]
What changes is the adequacy proof for the finite abstraction.  The old proof tried to
hand-build a global relation by local transformers.  The repaired proof represents the
normal form by finite patchwork bags and lets the RCC5 patchwork theorem provide global
amalgamation.  The automaton is used only to decide the existence of such a regular
patchwork-bag presentation and to manage infinite eventualities.

\section*{Remaining external input}

The repaired proof is self-contained except for two standard background results:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the patchwork property for complete atomic RCC5 networks;
\item decidability of emptiness for finite two-way alternating parity tree automata.
\end{enumerate}
If a completely self-contained manuscript is desired, the RCC5 patchwork theorem should
be proved in an appendix or cited precisely.  The referee explicitly recommended this
patchwork route, and it is now the central replacement for the former unproved
transformer-coherence lemma.

\section*{Bottom line}

The referee's report identified a genuine defect in the abstraction-adequacy layer.  The
repaired proof addresses it by replacing route-dependent transformer coherence with
patchwork amalgamation, exact tree-decomposition identity, finite shadow profiles, and a
two-way parity automaton.  This materially strengthens the proof and localizes the
remaining mathematical dependence to the standard RCC5 patchwork theorem and the standard
two-way parity-automaton emptiness theorem.
