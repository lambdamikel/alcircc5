\section*{Verdict}

The referee report is substantive and the main criticism is accepted.  The previous automata manuscript did not prove a finite representation invariant strong enough to justify its patch condition.  In particular, it used phrases such as ``represented target'' and ``represented triple'' without proving that every semantic target and every RCC5-relevant triple in the infinite unfolded split forest has a finite representative checked by the automaton.

I have repaired the proof by replacing the informal patchwork condition with an explicit finite frontier/profile construction and finite pair/triple product-state representatives.  The repaired proof is contained in
\[
\texttt{split\_forest\_automata\_repaired\_full\_proof.tex/pdf}.
\]

\section*{Key correction: product-state representatives}

The central change is the following.  The automaton no longer merely checks local bags and hopes that global triples are covered.  Each profile carries finite pair and triple product states.  For any two occurrences in the unfolded split tree, the pair representative is computed from their nearest common interface and the finite pair transformers along the two paths to the endpoints.  For any three occurrences, the triple representative is computed analogously.  The triple representative contains the three pair labels and is checked against the RCC5 composition table.

Therefore the proof now has an explicit finite route from local checks to global composition:
\[
\text{local transformer checks}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{finite representative for every global triple}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{global RCC5 composition}.
\]

\section*{Diagnostic examples addressed}

The repaired proof explicitly discusses the referee's diagnostic tests.

For
\[
  \exists\PP.(\exists\DR.A)\sqcap\forall\DR.\neg A,
\]
the product-state triple check forces
\[
  x\PP y,\quad y\DR z
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  x\DR z
\]
because $\Comp(\PP,\DR)=\{\DR\}$.  Universal propagation then forces $z:\neg A$, contradicting $z:A$.

For the incompatible split-superpart test, if
\[
  x\PP y,
  \qquad
  x\PP z,
\]
then
\[
  y\PPI x,
  \qquad
  x\PP z,
\]
and the product-state triple check enforces
\[
  \rho(y,z)\in\Comp(\PPI,\PP)=\{\EQ,\PP,\PPI,\PO\}.
\]
The universal restrictions on the first witness reject all four cases.  Equality splitting does not hide the conflict, because equality-port synchronization requires identical pair summaries to all third occurrences.

\section*{Remaining status}

The repaired manuscript is substantially stronger than the earlier automata proof.  It gives an explicit finite interface invariant rather than relying on an informal patch condition.  As usual for a complex decidability proof, the product-state frontier construction and equality-congruence proof remain the sections most deserving of independent review or mechanization.  I no longer see the specific defects identified in the referee report in the repaired version.
