TARGET W1 -- THE STEERING INDUCTION (Lemma 4.3, "Canonical completion")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The keystone. Lemma 4.3 extends the patchworked skeleton to a total
frame by assigning every non-co-patterned pair either its chain-forced
value or a horizontal member of its fold intersected with Safe. The
proof compresses two triangle cases whose full expansion is admitted to
be missing (Remark 4.4; Section 10, W1):

  W1a (old-detour case). The assigned value for (y, b) is claimed to lie
      in "every composition through old occurrences, because F(y,b) is
      computed along the tree path and A is globally consistent: any old
      detour composes to a superset of the tree-path fold." Attack this:
      construct a realized old structure A (a closed atomic network,
      buildable as a tree of clusters), a glued fresh pattern, and a
      configuration where a realized old edge rho(y,z) (e.g. vertical)
      together with z's already-assigned value pins v_y to a value
      OUTSIDE {canonical selector} -- or worse, outside F(y,b) cap Safe.
      The suspect claim is the superset relation between detour
      compositions and the tree-path fold; check whether it actually
      holds when the detour leaves and re-enters the separator's
      component, and whether the ASSIGNMENT ORDER (all fresh members of
      one pattern at once, then the next pattern) matters.
  W1b (two-fresh-member case). For fresh b, b' with pattern value
      N_k(b',b), the proof claims the horizontal choices for (y,b) and
      (y,b') compose because "the fold F(y,b) was computed through the
      same separator and the horizontal choice was taken inside F(y,b)."
      Verify or refute: the horizontal member of F(y,b) chosen need not
      be compatible with the horizontal member of F(y,b') chosen when
      Comp(chosen', N_k(b',b)) is a proper subset -- e.g. chosen' = DR,
      N_k(b',b) = PPI gives Comp(DR,PPI) = {DR}: is DR then guaranteed
      to be in F(y,b) cap Safe? (The manuscript's selector prefers DR
      when available -- find a configuration where it is NOT available
      for one pair but forced by the other pair's choice.)
  W1c (fixed-point definition of R(k,k') in (Q4)). The fold sets are
      computed "over separator-mediated paths in unfoldings" by a fixed
      point on the gluing graph. Check well-definedness: does the fixed
      point over-approximate (harmless) or can it UNDER-approximate the
      folds realizable in unfoldings (fatal for (Q4)'s coverage --
      e.g. paths through the CORE, which is shared by all patterns and
      creates shortcuts the gluing graph may not represent)? The core
      threading (Q5) makes every unfolding non-tree-like as a graph of
      shared occurrences; verify the fold analysis and the running-
      intersection claim survive core sharing.
