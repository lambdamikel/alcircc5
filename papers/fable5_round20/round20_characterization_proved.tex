\begin{abstract}

Round-19 left exactly one \code{sorry}: the characterization
obligation \code{uSource\_eq\_frame} --- on wellformed certificates,
the source-oriented update U1--U5 agrees with the operationally
unfolded frame on every distinct existing non-core-core pair.  That
statement, whose prose renditions produced defects \#11 and \#12, is
now a kernel-checked theorem: \code{formal/Round19Transport.lean}
builds with \textbf{zero} \code{sorry}, no axioms beyond Lean's
kernel, and no \code{native\_decide} anywhere in the main proof chain
(it remains only in the standalone witness examples).  The companion
fuel-adequacy obligation is discharged in the same file
(\code{uSourceFuel\_irrel}: the update is constant in the fuel above
the explicit threshold $2\cdot\mathrm{maxBirth}+1$, plus one on
flipped orientation), and non-vacuity is witnessed
(\code{certC\_wellformed} plus an end-to-end \code{example}).  The
mathematically substantive outcome is that the proof \emph{forced
four additional wellformedness clauses}, each semantically intended
since round 15 and each silently honored by the WP34 generator, none
previously stated.  Status: \emph{strongly supported, not certified}.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \code{frame\_char}: for every wellformed certificate and every
$n \le$ the number of steps, after $n$ canonical steps the frame value
of every distinct existing non-core-core pair equals \code{uSource}.
Plain induction on the canonical step list --- the induction the
thirteenth review's canonical-order determinism made available.  Four
cases per step: both endpoints old (first-match monotonicity), old
member/fresh (pattern value; the birth flip is
\code{net\_conv}), old steered/fresh (steering value; the recursive
row reconstruction matches the recorded rows by the induction
hypothesis plus fuel irrelevance), co-birth (pattern value --- defect
\#12's geometry, now one induction case).
\item \code{uSourceFuel\_irrel}: round-19's stated fuel-adequacy
obligation.  The recursion measure is made exact:
$\mathrm{fuelNeed} = 2\cdot\mathrm{maxBirth}+1$, plus one on the
younger-first orientation; above it the fuel is irrelevant.
\item \code{certC\_wellformed} and an \code{example} applying the
main theorem to a concrete certificate with an inherited slot:
\code{Wellformed} is not vacuous, and the theorem fires end to end.
\item Supporting layer: a first-match calculus for frame lookup
(membership plus value-uniqueness replaces all ordering reasoning), a
domain invariant (frame keys mention only existing occurrences), and
injectivity of the port map on member ports --- the usable form of the
twelfth review's 17.4 discipline.
\end{itemize}

\section{The four forced wellformedness clauses}

The proof would not close without four clauses round-19's ``minimal''
\code{Wellformed} lacked.  Each is a statement the manuscripts always
intended and the WP34 generator always satisfied; the medium made
their absence a type error rather than a review finding:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.2\linewidth}p{0.72\linewidth}}
\toprule
\code{targets\_exist} & Slot targets are \emph{real} occurrences
(in-range fresh indices).  Without it, ghost occurrences enter
patterns and pollute recorded rows.\\
\code{targets\_length} & Exactly one target per template slot.
Without it, out-of-range slot ports alias core~0 under the port map's
default and injectivity fails.\\
\code{net\_conv} & Pattern nets are converse-coherent --- the R2 half
of ``patterns are closed atomic networks''.  Without it the two
orientations of one pattern pair may disagree, and the birth flip
(round-18's vindicated clause) is unsound.\\
\code{f\_reads\_rows} & The steering function is a function \emph{of
the rows}: it reads only in-range core and slot columns.  Without it,
steering could distinguish the frame's padding from template junk
outside the interface, and the recursive row reconstruction of U3
would not determine the recorded value.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

This is the round-19 thesis operating at the next level: in prose,
each of these would have been a referee's defect \#13; in Lean, they
were unprovable-goal errors during round-20's development, fixed
before any reviewer saw them.
