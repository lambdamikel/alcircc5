Status: \emph{strongly supported, not certified}.

\section{What was proved}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \code{uSource\_conv}: converse coherence of the source-oriented
update (R2).  Definitional in every clause except the two crossing a
fuel boundary, which the round-20 fuel-irrelevance lemma bridges; the
co-birth case is exactly the \code{net\_conv} wellformedness clause
round-20 forced.
\item \code{pairVal} and \code{SCond}: the realized network of a
certificate, and the S-condition on it --- core net converse-coherent,
proper, closed, and agreeing with template core rows (N3); at every
step, every triangle through a fresh occurrence composition-closed,
and every fresh pair EQ-free.  All fields are checkable on a concrete
certificate; a \code{Decidable (p \(\to\) q)} instance (absent from
core Lean) makes the bounded quantifier chains \code{decide}-friendly.
\item \code{pairVal\_closed}: the abstract closure theorem.  The proof
is a \emph{max-birth rotation argument}: every distinct triple contains
an occurrence of maximal birth step; the S-condition covers the
triangle with that occurrence in the middle position, and the two
kernel-checked rotation facts \code{comp\_rot1}/\code{comp\_rot2}
(finite, by \code{decide}) together with converse coherence rotate it
into the goal orientation.  No induction beyond the trichotomy is
needed --- the birth structure of the fold does all the work.
\item \code{frame\_closed}, \code{frame\_conv}, \code{frame\_proper}:
the operational forms, by transport along round-20's
\code{uSource\_eq\_frame} plus a new core-row lemma
(\code{get?\_corecore}: core-core frame entries carry the core net,
because the first write is a pattern write whose core ports the N3
condition pins).
\item Witnesses: \code{certC\_scond} with an end-to-end closed-frame
\code{example}; \code{certD\_wellformed},
\code{certD\_scond\_violated} and \code{certD\_frame\_not\_closed}
(values $\mathrm{DR}/\mathrm{PPI}/\mathrm{PO}$ with
$\mathrm{DR} \notin \comp(\mathrm{PPI},\mathrm{PO})$) --- the
adversarial control of WP32 Part~C as kernel-checked theorems.
\end{itemize}

\section{What this closes, and what it does not}

Closed: the seam that produced defects \#10--\#12 (steering conditions
versus realized frames) is now theorem-bounded end to end ---
certificate syntax (\code{Wellformed}), realized values
(\code{uSource} = frame, round-20), and soundness of the S-condition
(closed proper atomic presentations, this round).  Not closed: the
catalogue level --- the manuscripts check S4 on finitely many
interface states and conclude closure for \emph{all} unfoldings of the
catalogue, while \code{Cert} encodes one canonical unfolding; lifting
\code{SCond} to a finite catalogue with a reachability fixed point is
the natural round-22.  Below everything, the standing completeness
mathematics (F6 width budgets, W2$'$ uniformization) and the single
external input (the RCC5 patchwork property) are untouched.  Ledger:
thirteen reviews --- twelve defects, one no-counterexample; rounds
19--21 unreviewed.  The decidability theorem for $\ALCIRCC$ remains
\textbf{strongly supported, not certified}.
