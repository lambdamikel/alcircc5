Observation:
  Even this one-state regular vertical chain has complete Gaifman graph K_n on every n-prefix.
  Any standard guarded/tree-decomposition argument that treats every RCC atom as a guard
  sees unbounded clique/tree width. The model is regular, but not guarded-tree-like
  in the ordinary sparse-Gaifman sense.

Conclusion:
  Off-the-shelf guarded/clique-guarded covers do not directly close the proof.
  The needed cover theorem must exploit finite edge colours and realized triple patterns,
  rather than bounded Gaifman/clique width.
